# Mask360 Performance Marketing System

Performance marketing at Mask360 is brand-aware paid media, every campaign must convert without cheapening the brand. ROAS matters. So does how the ad feels in the feed.

---

## Core Philosophy

For luxury brands: Performance marketing is always a servant of brand, not its replacement. A 5x ROAS on a discount ad that erodes positioning is a loss.

For premium/lifestyle brands: The funnel must feel seamless, the ad should feel like the brand, the landing page should feel like the ad, the purchase should feel like both.

Mask360 standard: Never recommend a campaign that optimises for volume at the cost of brand equity.

## Meta Ads: Full Framework

### Account Structure (Standard)

Campaign Level: Objective + Budget
  TOF Ad Sets (Cold audiences, awareness + consideration)
  MOF Ad Sets (Warm audiences, engaged, video viewers, page visitors)
  BOF Ad Sets (Hot audiences, retargeting, past customers)

### Audience Architecture

TOF (cold):
- Interest stacks: 3-5 tightly related interests, never broad single categories
- 1% Lookalike from best customers (not all customers)
- Broad (Advantage+): Let Meta find them, works well for lifestyle/aspirational brands

MOF (warm):
- Video viewers: 25% / 50% / 75% thresholds
- Instagram engagers: Last 30 / 60 / 90 days
- Website visitors: Segmented by page (product vs. editorial vs. checkout)

BOF (hot):
- Add-to-cart non-purchasers (last 14 days)
- Initiate-checkout non-purchasers (last 7 days)
- Past purchasers (upsell / repeat)
- Always exclude: Recent purchasers from TOF/MOF

### Bidding Strategy by Stage

| Stage | Bid Strategy |
|---|---|
| TOF awareness | Reach / ThruPlay / Lowest Cost |
| TOF traffic | CPC with landing page view optimisation |
| MOF | Lowest Cost / Cost Cap |
| BOF | Cost Cap / ROAS target (if data sufficient) |

### Budget Allocation

New accounts or launches:
- TOF: 60-70% / MOF: 20-25% / BOF: 10-15%

Mature accounts with strong retargeting pool:
- TOF: 50% / MOF: 20% / BOF: 30%

### Creative Testing Framework

Test one variable at a time, in this order of impact:
1. Hook / first frame (highest impact variable)
2. Format (static vs. video vs. carousel)
3. Copy angle (emotional vs. feature vs. social proof)
4. CTA text

Testing rules:
- Minimum 7 days before judging
- Minimum 50 link clicks or 1,000 impressions before comparing
- Kill underperformers at 2x CPA target without conversion

Winning creative signals:
- CTR: above 1.5% (luxury: 0.8%+ acceptable given tight targeting)
- Hook rate (3-sec views / impressions): above 30%
- Frequency: above 3.5 = creative refresh needed

## Google Ads: Full Framework

### Campaign Types by Goal

| Goal | Campaign Type |
|---|---|
| Brand / branded queries | Search: Exact + Phrase match only |
| Non-branded demand capture | Search: Broad with Smart Bidding |
| Visual awareness | Display / Performance Max |
| Brand storytelling | YouTube: TrueView / Bumper |
| Remarketing | Display Remarketing / YouTube retargeting |

### Search Structure

Exact match: Brand terms, high-intent product terms
Phrase match: Category terms with qualifier
Broad match: Only with Target CPA/ROAS + 30+ conversions/month data

Negative keywords: Run search term report weekly for first 4 weeks, monthly thereafter.

### Conversion Events (Luxury/Hospitality Standard)

Must-track events:
- Page view (all)
- Scroll depth (50%, 75%, 90%)
- Enquiry form submission
- Phone click / WhatsApp click
- Booking initiation
- Booking confirmation (primary conversion)

## Analytics & Reporting

### KPIs by Client Category

Luxury hospitality (Anantara, St. Regis):
- Primary: Booking enquiries, revenue generated
- Secondary: Cost per enquiry, ROAS on promo periods
- Track but don't optimise for: Reach, impressions, follower growth

Jewellery (ZORÁE, Kalyan):
- Primary: ROAS, revenue
- Secondary: Add-to-cart rate, checkout abandonment rate
- Watch: Average order value (LAL audiences should drive higher AOV)

Activewear (A29):
- Primary: ROAS, new customer acquisition cost
- Secondary: Repeat purchase rate from paid channels
- Watch: LTV:CAC ratio

Agency B2B prospecting (Mask360):
- Primary: Lead quality, not quantity
- Secondary: Cost per qualified conversation
- Watch: Attribution: most B2B is multi-touch

### Campaign Audit Checklist

1. Pixel firing correctly? All conversion events tracked?
2. Campaign structure: TOF/MOF/BOF separated or mixed?
3. Audience overlap: Ad sets competing against each other?
4. Creative fatigue: Frequency above 3.5? Last refresh date?
5. Budget allocation: BOF over-weighted vs. TOF?
6. Bidding: Strategy matched to account data maturity?
7. Placements: Manual or Advantage+? Audience Network on?
8. Attribution window: 7-day click / 1-day view set?
9. A/B tests: Any live? Properly isolated?
10. Reporting: Client seeing right metrics, not just vanity?

## Scaling Protocol

Rule 1: Never increase budget more than 20% at a time: Meta resets learning phase above this.
Rule 2: Scale by duplicating winning ad sets, not editing them.
Rule 3: Before scaling spend, scale creatives, more winners = more stable scaling.
Rule 4: Monitor frequency. As spend increases, frequency rises. Refresh creative before 3.5+.
Rule 5: Horizontal scaling (new audiences) before vertical (higher budgets in same audience).
