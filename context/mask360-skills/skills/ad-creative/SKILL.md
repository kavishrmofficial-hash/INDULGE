# Mask360 Ad Creative System

Performance creative at Mask360 lives at the intersection of brand aesthetics and conversion psychology. Luxury ads don't shout, they seduce.

---

## Core Philosophy

**Luxury ad creative never:**
- Uses urgency language ("Only 3 left!", "Act now!")
- Leads with price
- Feels like it's trying to sell something
- Includes more than one CTA
- Uses more than 10 words of on-screen text

**Luxury ad creative always:**
- Creates desire before explaining anything
- Leads with a feeling, not a feature
- Makes the viewer feel they're missing something by not being there
- Has a visual story even when the copy is 3 words

---

## Ad Formats by Platform

### Meta (Instagram / Facebook)

**Feed static:**
- Headline: 4-6 words max, no punctuation
- Body copy: 1-2 short sentences, emotional not transactional
- CTA button: "Learn More" or "Book Now" only (not "Shop Now" for ultra-luxury)

**Reel / video ad (15-30 sec):**
- First 3 seconds must hook without sound (many watch muted)
- Hook line (on screen): 3-5 words
- Mid-point visual reveal: the product or experience at its best
- End card: Logo + single line CTA

**Story ad:**
- Full screen: design for immersion
- Minimal text: let the visual do the work
- Swipe-up copy: 3-5 words, action-oriented

### Google Display / Programmatic

- Headline options (3-5 variations): Short, benefit-forward, no superlatives without proof
- Description: 1 sentence, evocative or specific, never generic
- Responsive display: Write 5 headlines + 3 descriptions, they mix-and-match

## Hook Formulas by Brand Category

**Hospitality (Anantara, St. Regis):**
- "Some places stay with you." *(nostalgia + desire)*
- "Jaipur is waiting." *(scarcity + place identity)*
- "Not every stay is a memory. This one is." *(loss aversion)*

**Jewellery (ZORÁE, Kalyan):**
- "For the moment that changes everything." *(emotional anchoring)*
- "She'll remember the feeling, not the occasion." *(identity)*
- "Lab-grown. Real forever." *(reframe + value)*

**Beauty / Fashion (Dior, L'Oréal):**
- "The rest is just skincare." *(superiority without shouting)*
- "Some things don't need explaining." *(luxury confidence)*
- "Your skin. Just elevated." *(identity alignment)*

**Activewear (A29):**
- "Train like you mean it." *(aspiration)*
- "The body you're building deserves this." *(identity + reward)*
- "Move different." *(brand positioning)*

## Neuromarketing Triggers for Ad Creative

| Trigger | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Loss aversion | Limited season, sold-out risk | "The monsoon retreat fills fast." |
| Social proof | When brand has credible names | "Worn by the women who lead." |
| Identity | Aspirational lifestyle brands | "This is what you're working toward." |
| Novelty | New product, new collection | "Nothing you've worn before." |
| Emotional anchoring | Occasion-based categories | "For the birthday she'll never forget." |
| Scarcity | Genuine limited availability | "Villa availability: April." |

## A/B Testing Logic for Luxury Clients

Always test these variables in order:

1. **Hook / first frame**: most impactful. Test 2-3 opening visuals or lines.
2. **Emotion vs. feature**: same product, one ad leads with feeling, one with specifics
3. **CTA copy**: "Book Now" vs. "Explore" vs. "Discover" for hospitality

Never test price positioning on luxury brands, it dilutes the category.

---

## Output Format

For any ad creative request, deliver:
1. **Primary ad**: the version to run first
2. **2 variants**: different hooks or angles, same visual intent
3. **Character counts**: flag anything that exceeds Meta/Google limits
4. **Notes**: one line on the psychology being applied, only if non-obvious
