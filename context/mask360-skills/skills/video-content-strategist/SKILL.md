# Mask360 Video Content Strategy System

Video at Mask360 is cinematic storytelling, not documentation. Even a 15-second reel should feel like a scene from a film the viewer wishes was longer.

---

## Video Philosophy

**For luxury brands:** Every frame is a frame. Composition, light, and pacing tell the story before the product appears.

**For agency showcase:** Mask360's video output should position the agency as the creative eye behind the work, not just the executor.

**For AI-generated video (Higgsfield/Kling):** Prompts are scripts. The model follows direction. Treat AI generation like briefing a cinematographer, camera, subject, movement, mood.

---

## Video Format Guide

### Instagram Reels (Primary format)

**Duration:** 15-30 seconds for luxury, up to 60 seconds for storytelling
**Structure:**
- 0-3s: Hook frame: visual pattern interrupt or text on screen
- 3-12s: Story or product reveal, the meat
- 12-25s: Emotional payoff or punchline
- 25-30s: Logo / tagline / CTA

**Text overlay rules:**
- Max 5 words per card
- Never explain what you're showing
- If visual is dramatic, make copy quiet. If soft, make copy sharp.

**Hook formats that work:**
- **The provocative question:** "What does Jaipur feel like at sunrise?"
- **The bold statement:** "We don't just shoot hotels. We make them feel."
- **The split-screen tension:** "DON'T FALL / IN LOVE" (let the viewer resolve it)
- **The cold open:** No text. Just a stunning frame. Hook through visual.

### YouTube (Long-form brand films)

**Duration:** 2-8 minutes for brand documentaries
**Structure:**
- Open with a question or scene (not a logo)
- Build through people, place, or process
- Brand appears naturally in context
- Close with a feeling, not a sales message

### AI-Generated Video (Higgsfield / Kling / WAN)

See the dedicated Higgsfield Prompting skill for full prompt architecture. Key principles:
- **Three-layer system:** Image prompt (keyframe) → Identity prompt (if characters needed) → Motion prompt (camera + subject action only)
- Video prompt: Short. 5-10 words. Camera verb + subject action.
- Common luxury hotel motions: dolly in, crane down, pan reveal, FPV walkthrough

## Reel Concept Formats

**Format 1: The Walkthrough**
Single location, one continuous movement through a space. Works for hospitality, interiors, retail.
Copyline: Minimal. Let the space speak.
AI approach: Start & End Frame in Kling, enter at exterior, exit inside.

**Format 2: The Split-Screen Tension**
Two visual panels carrying opposing or completing copy. "DON'T FALL / IN LOVE" style.
Works for: Agency showcases, brand launches, campaign teasers.

**Format 3: The Process Reveal**
Behind the creative, shoot day, kitchen prep, atelier work. Builds brand trust.
Copy: Real, raw, specific. Not curated.

**Format 4: The Testimonial Reimagined**
No talking head. Real customer moments reconstructed cinematically.
Copy: Their words, in text overlay. Minimal music.

**Format 5: The Concept Film**
No product. Pure mood. A feeling the brand owns.
Works for: Ultra-luxury launches, campaign openers.
Copy: One line. Or none.

## Agency Showcase Reel Rules (Mask360 posting own work)

When posting an Anantara reel, ZORÁE campaign, or any client work on @mask360.agency:

- Mask360 is the eye behind the work, position as creative director, not vendor
- Never caption "We worked with [Client]", let the work speak, add context subtly
- Show the craft: AI transitions, colour grade, copywriting, make the process visible
- Agency copy tone: Quietly arrogant. "This is our work." Not "We're so proud to share..."

## Output Format

For any video creative request:
1. **Concept**: the idea in one sentence
2. **Shot/frame breakdown**: scene by scene or card by card
3. **Text overlay copy**: exact words per frame
4. **Caption**: for the post that accompanies the video
5. **Higgsfield/AI prompt** (if AI generation is involved)
