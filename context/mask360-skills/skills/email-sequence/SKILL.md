# Mask360 Email Sequence System

Email at Mask360 is the most intimate marketing channel. It's the one place where a brand speaks directly, without an algorithm between them and the reader. Treat every email as a letter, not a broadcast.

---

## Email Philosophy

**For luxury brands:**
- Email is permission-based intimacy: never abuse it
- One idea per email, always
- Shorter is almost always better
- Design should feel editorial, not commercial
- Frequency: less than you think, more thoughtful than you imagine

**For premium / lifestyle brands:**
- Sequence logic matters more than individual email quality
- Each email should earn the right to the next one
- Every sequence has a narrative arc, not just a series of sends

---

## Sequence Types

### 1. Welcome Sequence (New subscriber / member)
**Goal:** Build relationship, not sell immediately
**Length:** 3-5 emails over 10-14 days

- **Email 1 (Immediate):** Welcome: who the brand is, what they can expect. Warm, not transactional.
- **Email 2 (Day 3):** Brand story, why the brand exists, the people behind it. Human, specific.
- **Email 3 (Day 7):** The world, lifestyle content, editorial, editorial edit. No hard sell.
- **Email 4 (Day 10):** Social proof or community, who else is here, what they love.
- **Email 5 (Day 14):** Soft CTA, first product or booking invite. Earned by now.

### 2. Launch Sequence (New collection / property / product)
**Goal:** Build anticipation → drive action
**Length:** 4-6 emails over 7-14 days

- **Teaser (Day -7):** "Something is coming." No details. Pure intrigue.
- **Reveal (Day -3):** The what, reveal the product/collection with editorial visuals.
- **Story (Day -1):** The why, the inspiration, the craft, the people behind it.
- **Launch (Day 0):** Now live, direct and clear. Link, hero image, CTA.
- **Social proof (Day +3):** Early reactions, press quotes, customer moments.
- **Last chance (Day +7):** If relevant, limited availability, closing period.

### 3. Nurture / Lifecycle Sequence (Ongoing database)
**Goal:** Maintain relationship, drive periodic engagement
**Frequency:** 2x/month maximum for luxury brands
**Content mix:** 70% value (editorial, stories, experiences) / 30% commercial

### 4. Blast Email (One-time campaign send)
**Goal:** A single, time-bound message, event, offer, announcement
**Rules:**
- One message. One CTA.
- Subject line under 50 characters
- Pre-header: continues the subject, adds intrigue
- Mobile-first design: most luxury readers open on iPhone

## Email Copy Rules

**Subject lines:**
- Under 50 characters
- No caps-lock urgency ("FINAL HOURS", "DON'T MISS")
- Specific over vague ("A suite in Jaipur. Yours for April." beats "Exclusive offer inside")
- Question format sparingly: works for hospitality and events

**Pre-header text:**
- Continues the subject: adds a second thought, not a repeat
- Under 80 characters

**Body copy:**
- Opening: Never "Dear [First Name],", go straight to the idea
- Paragraphs: Short. 2-3 sentences max per block.
- No bullet points in luxury emails, prose only
- One link = one CTA. Never list multiple CTAs.
- Closing: A thought, not "Best regards, The [Brand] Team"

**CTA language:**
- Luxury: "Reserve your stay" / "Discover the collection" / "Read more"
- Premium lifestyle: "Shop the edit" / "Explore" / "Get yours"
- Never: "Click here" / "Submit" / "Buy now"

## Output Format

For any email sequence request:
1. **Sequence map**: number of emails, timing, goal of each
2. **Full copy** for each email: Subject / Pre-header / Body / CTA
3. **Design notes**: hero image direction, layout intent (if relevant)
4. **Tone reminder**: one line on voice for each email in the sequence
