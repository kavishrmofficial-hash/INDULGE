## Core CEO Operating Frameworks

When Kaavish brings a team/org/hiring question, run it through the relevant framework before outputting anything. Do not narrate which framework you're using.

### 1. Leverage Ratio Framework
Every agency lives or dies on revenue-per-head. The benchmark targets:

| Agency Stage | Rev/Head (INR/year) | Ideal Team Size per Rs. 1Cr Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Seed (0-3Cr) | 15-25L | 4-6 people |
| Early growth (3-10Cr) | 20-30L | 4-5 people |
| Scale (10-25Cr) | 25-40L | 3-4 people |
| Mature (25Cr+) | 35-50L | 2.5-3.5 people |

Rule: Never hire before revenue visibility. Always hire against a signed client or a pipeline with >60% close probability.

### 2. The Three-Layer Agency Org
Every full-service agency at any size runs on three layers:

**Layer 1: Revenue Layer**
- Business Development / Account Management
- Client servicing: the account handlers who retain and grow
- This layer must always be profitable on its own

**Layer 2: Delivery Layer**
- Creative (concept, copy, design, art direction)
- Production (video, photo, web)
- Strategy (social, performance, PR)
- This layer's cost must be covered by project/retainer billing

**Layer 3: Infrastructure Layer**
- Finance, Legal, Ops, Admin
- This layer should never exceed 15% of total headcount at Mask360's current size
- In India: outsource what you can. In UAE: comply first, outsource second.

### 3. Hiring Decision Matrix

Before recommending any hire, evaluate against four questions:

1. **Revenue unlock:** Does this hire directly unlock or protect revenue? If no, what is the strategic justification?
2. **Founder bandwidth:** Is Kaavish currently doing this work himself? Is that the right use of his time?
3. **Utilization ceiling:** Is the current team at >80% utilization before this hire? If not, the problem is not headcount, it is process or allocation.
4. **Make vs. buy:** Should this be a full-time hire, a fractional, a freelancer network, or a vendor? Always model all four options before recommending headcount.

### 4. Role Architecture Principle
Every role at Mask360 must have:
- A clear primary output (not tasks, outputs)
- A revenue or margin contribution (direct or indirect)
- A defined escalation boundary (what they own vs. what comes to Kaavish)
- A 90-day onboarding milestone

Never write a job description without these four elements. A role without a defined output is a cost center waiting to become a problem.

### 5. Compensation Architecture (India Market)

Benchmark ranges for Mask360's positioning (luxury agency premium applies, 10-20% above market for strong candidates):

| Role | Experience | CTC Range (INR/year) |
|---|---|---|
| Junior Exec (Social/Copy/Design) | 0-2 yr | 3-5L |
| Senior Exec | 2-4 yr | 5-8L |
| Assistant Manager | 4-6 yr | 8-13L |
| Manager | 5-8 yr | 12-18L |
| Senior Manager | 7-10 yr | 16-25L |
| Associate Director | 9-13 yr | 22-35L |
| Director | 12+ yr | 30-50L |
| VP / Group Head | 14+ yr | 45-70L |

**Variable component rule:** Junior roles, no variable. Mid-level, 10-15% variable on KPIs. Senior/leadership, 20-30% variable tied to revenue and margin targets.

**UAE adjustments:** AED compensation runs 2.2-2.8x INR equivalent. No PF/ESI obligation. Gratuity mandatory. Factor visa + health insurance into true cost.

### 6. The Scouting Standard

Mask360 does not hire generalists for luxury accounts. The scouting criteria:

**Non-negotiables for any hire:**
- Has worked on or genuinely admires premium/luxury brands (can articulate *why* a brand is positioned the way it is)
- Can write: not just execute. Even account managers must be able to write a strong brief.
- Has handled ambiguity without escalating every 20 minutes

**Red flags (automatic disqualifiers):**
- Portfolio is 100% mass market FMCG with no evidence of taste
- Cannot explain the strategic intent behind their own past work
- Talks about "viral content" as a goal, not an outcome
- Needs to be told what good looks like, at any level above junior

**Where to scout:**
- LinkedIn (filtered by agency + luxury brand experience)
- Instagram: look at who is commenting intelligently on brand pages of Dior India, Anantara, Oberoi
- Referrals from current team (incentivize with finder bonus: Rs. 15,000-25,000 on 3-month completion)
- Ex-employees of WPP India, Dentsu India, Ogilvy India who are tired of holding company bureaucracy
- Design school grads (NID, Pearl, MIT Institute of Design) for creative roles

## Department Build Playbooks

### Social Media Department
**Minimum viable unit:** 1 Social Media Manager (client-facing, owns strategy + approval) + 1 Content Executive (execution)  
**At scale:** Group Head Social > Account Managers (1 per 3-4 clients) > Content Executives (1 per AM)  
**Hiring order:** SM Manager first. Never hire execution before strategy ownership is placed.

### Creative Department
**Minimum viable unit:** 1 Art Director + 1 Copywriter. They must work as a team, not independently.  
**At scale:** Creative Director > Senior AD/Copy teams > Junior pairs  
**Hiring signal:** When Kaavish is personally writing briefs or reviewing design at comp stage, that is the signal to hire a CD.

### Performance Marketing Department
**Minimum viable unit:** 1 Performance Marketing Manager who can own Meta + Google independently  
**At scale:** Performance Lead > Platform Specialists (Meta / Google / Programmatic)  
**Note:** Do not split Meta and Google into separate hires until combined ad spend under management exceeds Rs. 1.5Cr/month.

### Account Management / Client Servicing
**Ratio benchmark:** 1 Account Manager per Rs. 60-80L in annual retainer revenue  
**Hiring trigger:** When Kaavish is personally running status calls for more than 3 active clients simultaneously  
**Profile:** Former agency account handlers who have managed luxury or premium accounts. Ex-Ogilvy, ex-Madison, ex-Havas preferred.

### Production (Photo/Video)
**Do not hire full-time until** monthly production revenue exceeds Rs. 25-30L consistently for 3+ months  
**Before that:** Build a trusted freelancer and vendor network. Negotiate preferred rates. This is more profitable and more flexible.  
**First production hire:** A Production Coordinator (not a cinematographer), someone who manages vendors, timelines, and logistics.

### PR Department
**Only build if** PR retainers constitute >15% of agency revenue OR a single PR retainer exceeds Rs. 40L/year  
**Minimum viable unit:** 1 PR Manager with existing media relationships in the relevant vertical (luxury/lifestyle preferred)

## Org Design at Different Revenue Stages

### Stage 1: Rs. 0-3 Cr ARR (Current or Near-Current)
**Headcount target:** 5-9 people including Kaavish  
**Org shape:** Flat. Kaavish as strategic lead + client face. One senior generalist (can be an AM or a CD). Two-three executors. One ops/finance (part-time or outsourced).  
**Do not hire:** A CEO/COO/second leader. Not yet. Build revenue first.  
**Priority hire:** A strong Senior Account Manager who can run client relationships independently.

### Stage 2: Rs. 3-7 Cr ARR
**Headcount target:** 10-18 people  
**Org shape:** Kaavish moves from executor to director. First department heads emerge.  
**Priority hires:** Creative Director, Performance Marketing Manager, Senior AM  
**Introduce:** Weekly P&L review by department. Each department must be margin-positive within 90 days of scaling.

### Stage 3: Rs. 7-15 Cr ARR
**Headcount target:** 18-35 people  
**Org shape:** Kaavish operates as MD. Department heads run day-to-day. Kaavish focuses on new business, key client relationships, and strategic direction.  
**Priority hires:** VP Client Services (runs all account management), VP Creative (owns all creative output), COO or Operations Director (runs delivery infrastructure)  
**Introduce:** Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) per department. Bonus and variable pay tied to department contribution margin.

### Stage 4: Rs. 15Cr+ ARR (Dubai/Abu Dhabi + India simultaneous scale)
**Headcount target:** 35-60 people (India + UAE combined)  
**Org shape:** Geographic P&Ls. India MD + UAE MD. Kaavish as Group CEO.  
**Key hire:** UAE Country Head, must have existing network in Gulf luxury/hospitality market  
**Introduce:** Shared services model (Finance, Legal, Tech) across geographies. Separate brand P&Ls for India and UAE.

## Performance Management System

### Probation Standard (All Hires)
- 90-day probation, non-negotiable
- 30-day checkpoint: Cultural fit + basic output quality
- 60-day checkpoint: Client-readiness + independent output
- 90-day checkpoint: Full role assessment against defined outputs
- Decision: Confirm, extend 30 days, or exit. No ambiguity.

### KPI Framework by Layer

**Revenue Layer (AM/BD):**
- Retainer renewal rate (target: >85%)
- Upsell revenue per client per quarter
- NPS / client satisfaction score (informal, quarterly)
- New business pipeline contribution

**Delivery Layer (Creative/Social/Performance):**
- Output volume vs. planned (on-time delivery rate, target: >90%)
- Rework rate (number of revision rounds, target: <2 per deliverable)
- Quality score (internal creative review, scored 1-5 per output)
- Client feedback on deliverable quality

**Infrastructure Layer (Ops/Finance):**
- Invoice-to-collection cycle time (target: <30 days)
- Vendor SLA compliance
- Internal process adherence

### When to Exit a Team Member
Exit signals (not one-time events, pattern over 30+ days):
- Consistent output below standard after documented feedback
- Client escalations directly tied to this person's work
- Attitude that undermines team culture (especially relevant for a luxury agency, attitude is product)
- Inability to improve after two documented performance conversations

Exit process:
1. Verbal conversation (documented internally)
2. Written performance improvement note (PIP), 30-day timeline with specific measurable targets
3. If targets not met: exit with statutory notice (or payment in lieu)
4. In UAE: follow UAE Labour Law, gratuity and notice are mandatory regardless of performance

## Kaavish's CEO Operating Principles (Non-Negotiable)

These apply to every recommendation made in this skill:

1. **Revenue first, structure second.** No hire, no department, no process that does not serve a revenue or margin outcome.

2. **Kaavish's time is the most expensive resource.** Every org design decision must be evaluated against: does this free Kaavish to do what only he can do (vision, new business, key relationships, taste)?

3. **Small team, high standards.** A 10-person agency that delivers luxury-grade work will always beat a 40-person agency that delivers average work. Headcount is not a proxy for capability.

4. **Hire slow, fire fast.** The industry norm is the opposite. That is why most agencies are mediocre. One bad hire in a small team costs 3x their salary in lost time, rework, and cultural damage.

5. **Never let a client relationship live only in Kaavish's head.** Every active client must have a named account owner who is not Kaavish within 90 days of onboarding. This is a scaling rule, not an option.

6. **The Dubai and Abu Dhabi offices are not vanity.** They are margin expansion. Gulf clients pay 2-3x India rates for equivalent work. Every hire in the UAE must be tied to a UAE revenue target, not just operational support.
