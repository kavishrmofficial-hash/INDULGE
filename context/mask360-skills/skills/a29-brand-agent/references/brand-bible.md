# A29 Wellbeing: Brand Bible
## For internal Mask360 use only

---

## Brand Positioning Statement

A29 Wellbeing is India's premium movement-first lifestyle brand, built for people who treat wellness as identity, not obligation. Where Alo Yoga made wellness aspirational in the West, A29 is doing the same for India's urban wellness generation: the 25-35-year-old in Mumbai who does reformer pilates before work, the Delhi woman who goes from gym to brunch without changing, the Bangalore man who takes recovery as seriously as performance.

**One-line brand truth:** *Movement is not what you do. It's who you are.*

---

## Target Persona

### Primary: "The Urban Wellness Native"
- **Age:** 24-34
- **Gender:** Women-primary, men-secondary
- **Cities:** Mumbai (Bandra, Worli, Juhu), Delhi (Hauz Khas, South Delhi, Vasant Kunj), Bangalore (Indiranagar, Whitefield, Koramangala)
- **Income:** ₹80k-₹2.5L/month household
- **Lifestyle:** Gym 4x/week, follows pilates/yoga trends, drinks oat milk, plans fitness retreats, browses Alo/Lululemon but buys locally when quality matches
- **Digital:** Instagram-primary. Saves aesthetically pleasing posts. Follows 5-10 fitness creators. Reposts wellness quotes unironically.
- **Pain point:** Indian activewear brands feel cheap or over-branded. International brands don't always understand the Indian body or the Indian heat.
- **What they want from A29:** A brand that looks like it could be international, because *they* feel international.

### Secondary: "The Fitness-Curious Aspirant"
- **Age:** 21-27
- **Tier 2 cities entering fitness culture:** Pune, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh
- **Aspirational buyer:** buys one piece as an entry point
- **Content hook:** Community, #MOVETOGETHER, influencer-driven discovery

## Competitive Landscape

| Brand | Positioning | A29's Edge |
|---|---|---|
| **Alo Yoga** | Global premium, celebrity-adjacent | A29 is Alo for India, same aesthetic, no import tax, culturally rooted |
| **Lululemon** | Performance-first, technical | A29 is lifestyle-first, more editorial |
| **Blissclub** | India's women's activewear, body-positive | A29 is more premium, more aspirational, less cause-driven |
| **HRX** | Mass market, celebrity (Hrithik) | A29 is niche, quality-forward, no celebrity dependency |
| **Decathlon** | Utility, price point | Completely different customer |

**A29's white space:** Premium Indian activewear with *international visual language* and *local cultural intelligence*. Nobody owns this yet.

## Brand Architecture

### The Three Brand Promises
1. **Fit for movement, made for life**: technical enough to perform, beautiful enough to live in
2. **Rooted in India, built for the world**: colour names, cultural calendar, local influencers, but global aesthetic standards
3. **Community before commerce**: MOVE TOGETHER isn't a slogan, it's a product strategy

### Visual Identity System
- **Primary palette:** Soil (warm brown), Coal (near-black), Sand (off-white/beige), Sage (muted green)
- **Editorial palette:** High-contrast white/black for studio shots
- **Lifestyle palette:** Golden hour amber, soft morning light, green outdoor environments
- **Typography feel:** Clean serifs or minimalist sans-serif, never bold/aggressive
- **Photography:** Natural light preferred. Models in motion or at ease, never posed stiffly. Real sweat acceptable. Joy over perfection.

### Tone of Voice Spectrum
```
Aspirational ←------------→ Accessible
     [Feed posts]    [Stories]    [Community/DMs]
```

Feed posts live at the aspirational end. Stories can be warmer. DMs/comments should feel like a real person.

## What A29 Is Not

- Not a gym brand. Not a supplement brand. Not a diet culture brand.
- Not loud. Not aggressive. Not discount-driven.
- Not trying to be Lululemon. Not trying to be Blissclub.
- Not for people who just want cheap workout clothes.

A29 is for people who *invest* in how they feel.
