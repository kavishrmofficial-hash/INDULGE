# A29 Campaign Engine
## For collection launches, big ideas, and brand campaigns

---

## Campaign Architecture: The 4-Layer Model

Every A29 campaign, big or small, runs through these four layers:

```
LAYER 1: THE BIG IDEA
         ↓
LAYER 2: CREATIVE TERRITORIES (3 executional directions)
         ↓
LAYER 3: PLATFORM ROLLOUT (Reels + Feed + Stories + Ads)
         ↓
LAYER 4: INFLUENCER LAYER (Micro seeding + 1 macro amplification)
```

---

## Layer 1: Big Idea Framework

A strong A29 campaign idea answers one question:
**"What does this collection FEEL like, and what does wearing it say about who you are?"**

Not: "This collection has great fabric."
Yes: "This collection is for the woman who moves before the city wakes up."

### Naming campaigns
Campaign names follow A29's poetic naming convention:
- Draw from: nature, time of day, emotional states, Indian landscape
- Examples: *The Golden Hours Campaign*, *Before The City Wakes*, *Move Like Water*, *The Quiet Ones*, *Second Skin*
- Never name a campaign after a product feature ("The Breathable Collection")

---

## Layer 2: Creative Territories

Always present **3 creative territories** for any campaign, each with:
- Territory name
- Core insight
- Visual world
- Copy direction
- Lead Reel concept

### Territory Template:

**TERRITORY [#]: [NAME]**
- **Insight:** [The human truth this territory taps into]
- **Visual world:** [Lighting, location, model energy, colour palette, time of day]
- **Copy direction:** [The sentence-level tone, poetic? raw? quiet? declarative?]
- **Lead Reel:** [Single Reel concept, premise, action, music feel, caption]
- **Higgsfield angle:** [Which shot types / camera moves bring this territory to life]

---

## Layer 3: Platform Rollout for a Collection Launch

### 3-Post Launch Arc (minimum)

**POST 1: THE TEASE (3 days before launch)**
- Format: Reel, 15-20s
- Concept: Movement-forward. Don't show the product clearly. Show the *feeling*.
- Caption style: One sentence. A question or a statement that creates curiosity.
- Visual: Fast cuts, hands in fabric, movement blur, earth-tone palette
- Higgsfield: WAN 2.6 or Kling 3.0 multi-shot

**POST 2: THE LAUNCH**
- Format: Carousel (5-7 slides) + Reel version
- Slide 1: Hero product shot, clean, editorial
- Slides 2-4: Detail shots (fabric texture, movement, colorways)
- Slide 5: Model lifestyle shot
- Slide 6: Collection name card + shop CTA
- Caption: Full storytelling format: hook → product truth → emotional payoff → CTA
- Higgsfield: Veo 3.1 for premium 4K product hero

**POST 3: SOCIAL PROOF (3-5 days after launch)**
- Format: UGC compilation Reel or customer quote carousel
- Caption: Community-voice. Let the customer speak.
- If no UGC yet: Use a micro-influencer piece as social proof

---

## Layer 4: Influencer Campaign Brief Template

See `influencer-system.md` for full brief format.

For a campaign, always plan:
- **2-4 micro-influencers** (25k-100k): seeded with product, briefed but creatively free
- **1 macro influencer** (100k-500k): co-created content, more strategic alignment needed
- **Timing:** Micro go first (3 days before/during launch), macro amplifies at peak

## Evergreen Campaign Ideas (Ready to Deploy)

These can be activated any month with minimal modification:

### 1. "BEFORE THE CITY WAKES"
- **Insight:** The 5-7am window is sacred to the Indian fitness person. It's the only hour that's theirs.
- **Visual:** Pre-dawn Mumbai/Delhi. Marine Drive, Lodhi Garden. Lone runner. Warm street lights.
- **Copy:** "The city hasn't started yet. You already have."
- **Best for:** Monsoon, winter, January reset

### 2. "SECOND SKIN"
- **Insight:** The best activewear disappears, you forget you're wearing it.
- **Visual:** Extreme fabric close-ups, hands pulling at fabric, model looking down at themselves with quiet satisfaction
- **Copy:** "You'll forget you're wearing it. Until someone asks."
- **Best for:** New collection launch, product hero campaign

### 3. "REST IS MOVEMENT TOO"
- **Insight:** Recovery, rest days, and slow Sundays are part of the A29 lifestyle, not the absence of it.
- **Visual:** Cosy indoor. Sunlight through curtains. A29 joggers. Coffee. No gym in sight.
- **Copy:** "The outfit you wear when you're not working out says just as much."
- **Best for:** Joggers/lounge push, Mindset pillar

### 4. "MOVE TOGETHER" (Community Campaign)
- **Insight:** Fitness is more fun, consistent, and meaningful with others.
- **Visual:** Group workouts, friends post-class, sweat together
- **Copy:** "Find your people. Then move with them."
- **Best for:** Quarterly community push, influencer collab content
