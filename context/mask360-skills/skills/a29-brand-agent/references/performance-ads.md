# A29 Performance Ads System
## Meta (Instagram + Facebook): Conversion-focused

---

## Core Philosophy

Performance ads for A29 must do one thing brand content cannot: **create urgency without destroying aspiration.**

The A29 brand voice stays aspirational. But ads need to convert. The bridge:
- **Brand ads:** Make them want to be the person who wears A29
- **Performance ads:** Make them click because not clicking feels like a loss

## The Hook Architecture

Every A29 performance ad opens with one of these hook types. Rotate, never use the same hook type back-to-back in an ad set.

### Hook Type 1: The Mirror Hook
Make the viewer see themselves.
> "If you're still wearing the same gym set from 2021, this is your sign."
> "Your workout is better than your gym clothes. Let's fix that."
> "POV: You finally found activewear that keeps up with you."

### Hook Type 2: The Insider Hook
Give them information that feels exclusive.
> "The set that's been selling out every week. Here's why."
> "Most Indian activewear fits badly in the waist. Here's what A29 does differently."
> "What happens when you wear the same set 4 days in a row? (We tested it.)"

### Hook Type 3: The Identity Hook
Speak to who they want to be.
> "Not gym wear. A whole lifestyle."
> "The woman who wears A29 doesn't skip leg day. She also doesn't skip brunch."
> "For the ones who take their rest day as seriously as their training day."

### Hook Type 4: The Loss Aversion Hook
Make inaction feel like a cost.
> "The Rosewood Collection. Low stock, no restocks."
> "Everyone in your gym class is asking where she got her set."
> "This colorway sold out in 48 hours last time. Heads up."

## Full Ad Script Template

**FORMAT: 15s Reel Ad**

```
[0-2s] HOOK: Pattern interrupt. Text overlay + visual that stops scroll.
[2-8s] BODY: Show the product in use. Real movement. Real light.
         One key benefit, shown not said.
[8-12s] PROOF: Price anchor OR social proof OR UGC clip
[12-15s] CTA: "Shop the Lunara Collection" / "Link in bio" / "Only X left"
```

**FORMAT: Static Image Ad**

```
HEADLINE: [Hook, 5-8 words max]
SUBTEXT: [Product name + key benefit, 1 line]
VISUAL: [Product on model, lifestyle setting, earth-tone palette]
CTA BUTTON: "Shop Now" / "Explore Collection"
```

**FORMAT: Carousel Ad (5 cards)**

```
Card 1: Hook image + headline (scroll-stopper)
Card 2: Product front-on, clean editorial
Card 3: Product detail / texture / fabric close-up
Card 4: Lifestyle, model in motion / real setting
Card 5: Collection range + price + CTA
```

## Targeting Framework

### Cold audience (awareness → interest)
- **Interest targeting:** Yoga, Pilates, Fitness, Lululemon, Alo Yoga, Athleisure, Gymshark, Health & Wellness
- **Demographic:** Women 22-38, Tier 1 Indian cities, household income top 25%
- **Creative:** Identity hook or Mirror hook. Show lifestyle over product.

### Warm audience (retargeting: visited site, engaged with profile)
- **Creative:** Loss aversion hook. Specific product they viewed. Limited stock messaging.
- **Ad format:** Single image or short Reel, 7-10s

### Hot audience (abandoned cart, past purchasers)
- **Creative:** Social proof or new colorway. "You already know the quality."
- **Offer:** Free shipping reminder or new collection alert

---

## A/B Test Protocol

Always test two variables max per ad set:

**Test 1:** Hook type (Mirror vs Identity vs Loss Aversion)
**Test 2:** Visual style (Studio editorial vs Outdoor lifestyle vs UGC-style)

Winning creative in 7 days → scale the winner, kill the loser, iterate one new variable.

## Performance Benchmarks (India activewear, Instagram)

| Metric | Baseline target | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (link) | >1.2% | >2.5% |
| CPM | ₹80-₹150 | <₹80 |
| CPC | <₹25 | <₹12 |
| ROAS | >2.5x | >4x |
| Add to cart rate | >3% | >6% |

If CTR is low: problem is the hook, rewrite it.
If CTR is high but conversion low: problem is the landing page or price expectation, not the ad.
