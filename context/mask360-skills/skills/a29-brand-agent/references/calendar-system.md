# A29 Content Calendar System

## Calendar Output Format

When building a content calendar, always output:
1. **Month overview**: theme + big moment
2. **Weekly breakdown**: 4 weeks, 3-4 posts/week (12-16 posts/month)
3. **Each post**: pillar, format, visual direction, caption, Higgsfield prompt
4. **Reel-to-Feed ratio**: aim for 60% Reels, 40% Feed (carousels + singles)

---

## The Weekly Architecture (Repeatable Every Week)

| Day | Format | Pillar | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Reel | Movement | Start-of-week energy. Body in motion. |
| Wednesday | Feed Post / Carousel | Product | Mid-week desire. Specific product/collection. |
| Friday | Reel | Mindset | End-of-week reflection. The inner game. |
| Saturday/Sunday | Story series + 1 Feed | Community | MOVE TOGETHER energy. UGC reposts. |

This is the baseline skeleton. Layer campaign moments on top.

## Seasonal Content Pushes

### Summer (April-June)
- Focus: shorts, sports bras, vests, breathability
- Visual: bright outdoor, golden light, sweat is okay
- Copy hook: "The heat doesn't stop you."

### Monsoon (July-September)
- Focus: indoor content, joggers, mindset
- Visual: window light, rain outside, cosy but active
- Copy hook: "Some of the best workouts happen when the world slows down."

### Festive (October-November)
- Focus: matching sets as gifting, Diwali collection if applicable
- Visual: warm amber tones, celebration-adjacent
- Copy hook: "The one thing you'll actually wear after Diwali."

### Winter (December-February, Delhi-skewed)
- Focus: layering, joggers, jackets if added
- Visual: cold morning runs, misty parks
- Copy hook: "Coldest mornings. Best workouts."

## Content Calendar Rules

1. **Never post two product posts back to back.** Always break with a Movement or Mindset post.
2. **At least 1 Reel per week minimum.** Algorithm demands it.
3. **Stories are daily.** Calendars only plan Feed + Reels. Stories are always-on.
4. **UGC goes into Community slot.** Reposts from micro-influencers or customers count as community content.
5. **Collection launches get 3-post arcs:** Tease (Mindset/Movement adjacent) → Launch (Product) → Social proof (Community/UGC).
