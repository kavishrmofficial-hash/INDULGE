# A29 Influencer System
## Strategy, briefs, and UGC direction

---

## Influencer Philosophy

A29 does not use influencers as billboards. Influencers are **community proof**: real people who genuinely move, and happen to wear A29 while doing it.

The Alo Yoga model: seed to real practitioners, let the content be authentic, amplify what works.

**The A29 rule:** If it looks like an ad, it failed.

---

## Influencer Tier Strategy

### Tier 1: Micro Creators (25k-100k followers)
**Role:** Authenticity engine. Volume. Trust-building.
**What they post:** Real workouts, styling reels, "What I wore this week", morning routines
**Briefing style:** Loose. Direction, not script.
**Deliverables:** 1 Reel + 2 Stories minimum
**Compensation:** Product gifting (₹8,000-₹12,000 product value) + optional flat fee ₹5,000-₹15,000
**Volume:** 4-6 micro creators per month in steady state

**Profile to look for:**
- India-based, Tier 1 cities
- Fitness/lifestyle/wellness niche (pilates, yoga, gym, running)
- Engagement rate >4% (not follower-count obsessed)
- Feed aesthetic that could coexist with A29 content
- NOT: food bloggers, travel-only, fashion-only (unless fitness-adjacent)

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier Creators (100k-500k followers)
**Role:** Reach amplifier. 1-2 per quarter for campaign moments.
**What they post:** Coordinated campaign content, more polished, more strategic
**Briefing style:** Co-created. We bring the concept, they bring the personality.
**Deliverables:** 1 hero Reel + 3-4 Stories + 1 Feed post
**Compensation:** Flat fee ₹30,000-₹80,000 + product
**Volume:** 1 per major campaign

## UGC Direction for Creators

When briefing UGC-style content (for use in ads), add this to the brief:

> "We may want to use your content in our Instagram ads. By posting and tagging us, you give us permission to boost your content as a paid post. You will always be credited."

### UGC shot list to share with creators:
1. **The get-ready shot**: putting on the set, pulling up leggings, adjusting bra strap
2. **The movement shot**: squat, run, stretch, jump, fabric in motion
3. **The texture shot**: hands on fabric, close-up of waistband, seam detail
4. **The lifestyle shot**: post-workout coffee, walking out of gym, in the car
5. **The mirror shot**: GRWM energy, looking at themselves with quiet confidence

## Influencer Discovery Criteria

When identifying new creators to approach, evaluate on:

| Criterion | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Content quality | Could this post appear on A29's feed? |
| Engagement authenticity | Real comments, not "Great pic!" bots |
| Fitness credibility | Do they actually work out? |
| Aesthetic alignment | Earth tones, clean backgrounds, natural light |
| Audience location | Majority India, Tier 1 cities |
| Brand safety | No controversial content, no competing activewear exclusives |

### Red flags: do not approach:
- Creators who post a new brand every week (no loyalty signal)
- Creators whose engagement rate dropped >40% in 3 months
- Creators who post flat-lay product shots only
- Creators with no visible workout/movement content

## Monthly Influencer Reporting

Track for every creator:
- Reel views + reach
- Story views + swipe-ups
- Profile visits generated
- Follower referrals (tracked via UTM or discount code if used for tracking only, not for public discount)
- Content quality score (internal: 1-5 on aesthetic + alignment)

Best-performing creators from each month → priority for next campaign.
