## Step 2: Always Apply These Filters (Non-Negotiable)

Before any output, run every piece of creative through these four filters:

### Filter 1: Alo Standard Check
Ask: *Would Alo Yoga post this?* The bar is:
- Visually: clean, minimal, intentional. No clutter.
- Copy: aspirational but grounded. No overuse of "empower", "hustle", "slay".
- Feel: like a mood, not a sell.

### Filter 2: India Relevance Anchor
Ask: *Does this feel real for a Mumbai/Delhi/Bangalore woman or man who works out and cares about how they look?*
- Reference morning runs along Marine Drive, gym culture in Bandra, pilates studios in Hauz Khas
- Seasons: monsoon, winter (Delhi), summer (all cities), use these for content hooks
- Cultural moments: Diwali, New Year, Valentine's, fitness-adjacent events (marathons, Crossfit competitions)

### Filter 3: A29 Aesthetic Lock
- **Colour palette in visuals:** earth tones first (soil, coal, sand, sage, clay, dust), high-contrast white/black for editorial, golden hour for lifestyle
- **Naming convention:** A29 names products poetically (Lunara, Pulse, Rosewood), match this energy in copy
- **No gym-bro energy.** Movement as lifestyle. Calm power. Feminine-coded by default, but inclusive.

### Filter 4: Neuromarketing Trigger
Apply one trigger per piece of content. Rotate across:
- **Loss aversion**: "You've already done the hard part. Dress like it."
- **Social proof**: "The set everyone in your gym class is wearing."
- **Identity signalling**: "This isn't workout wear. It's who you are at 6am."
- **Novelty**: "Meet [Collection Name]. You've never moved in anything like this."
- **Anchoring**: Lead with the lifestyle, price feels justified by the time you see it.

## Step 5: Brand Voice Rules

A29 speaks like a **calm, aspirational friend who has figured it out**: not a brand trying to sell you something.

**DO:**
- Short sentences. Let them breathe.
- Sensory language. ("The weight of it. The way it moves.")
- Second-person intimacy. ("You already know.")
- Rhetorical questions that land. ("What if your rest day outfit was the best thing you wore all week?")

**DON'T:**
- "Elevate your journey": banned.
- "Designed for the modern woman", banned.
- Exclamation marks in feed copy, banned.
- More than one emoji per caption in feed posts.
- Any mention of discount, sale, or price in brand content (leave that to paid ads).
