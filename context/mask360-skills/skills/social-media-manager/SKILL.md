# Mask360 Social Media Strategy System

Social media strategy at Mask360 is about positioning, not posting frequency. Luxury and premium brands don't win by posting more, they win by being unmistakable when they do.

---

## Strategy Philosophy

**For luxury brands:**
Scarcity > frequency. Desire > information. Identity > features.
The brand is a world. Social media is a window into that world, not a catalog.

**For premium mass-market brands:**
Consistency + emotional resonance. The brand must feel the same across every touchpoint. Content is a trust-building mechanism, not a sales funnel.

**For Mask360's own Instagram:**
Platform purpose: talent attraction + industry positioning: NOT direct business development.
Audience: Creative directors, marketing managers, brand founders, agency peers.
Tone: The agency that thinks differently. Shows the work without explaining the work.

---

## Platform Selection Framework

| Platform | Use When |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Visual-first brand, aspirational product, Gen Z/Millennial audience, hospitality, fashion, beauty, lifestyle |
| LinkedIn | B2B component, C-suite audience, Mask360's own agency positioning, employer brand |
| YouTube | Long-form brand storytelling, tutorials, documentary-style content |
| Pinterest | Interiors, weddings, fashion, food: high-intent discovery |
| WhatsApp Broadcast | Direct community, high-loyalty customers, event updates |
| X/Twitter | Not recommended for luxury clients. Situational for cultural moments |

---

## Content Pillar Architecture (Standard)

Build every client strategy around 5 content pillars. Proportion varies by brand:

1. **Hero / Product** (20-30%): the brand's core offering at its most powerful
2. **Story / Heritage** (15-20%): origin, craft, people, values
3. **Lifestyle / World** (25-35%): the life the customer aspires to through this brand
4. **Behind the Scenes** (10-15%): process, production, team, creative
5. **Cultural / Moment** (10-15%): seasonal, trending, occasion-based

| Category | Instagram Feed | Stories | Reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ultra-luxury (Dior, St. Regis) | 3-4x/week | Daily (minimal) | 2x/week max |
| Premium hospitality (Anantara) | 4-5x/week | Daily | 3x/week |
| Jewellery (ZORÁE, Kalyan) | 4x/week | 3-5x/week | 2x/week |
| Activewear (A29) | 5-6x/week | Daily | 4x/week |
| Agency (Mask360) | 3x/week | 2-3x/week | 1-2x/week |

Quality > frequency. A missed day is better than a weak post.

## Growth Strategy Principles

**For luxury brands:** Don't chase followers, chase the right 10,000. Niche + premium positioning > mass reach.

**Influencer approach:**
- Tier 1 luxury: Macro lifestyle creators (500K+), very selective, long-form partnerships
- Premium/lifestyle: Mid-tier (50K-200K) + micro (10K-50K), better engagement, more authentic
- Always: aesthetic alignment over follower count

**Engagement strategy:**
- Respond to every DM within 24hrs
- Comments: Always reply with substance, never with emoji-only
- Story polls/questions: Use to gather data, not just boost engagement

## Social Audit Framework

When auditing a client's existing social presence, evaluate:

1. **Profile completeness**: bio, link, highlights, story covers
2. **Visual consistency**: grid aesthetic, colour palette, font usage
3. **Voice consistency**: does every caption sound like the same brand?
4. **Content mix**: are all 5 pillars represented?
5. **Engagement rate**: benchmark by category (luxury: 1-3% is excellent)
6. **Posting frequency**: is it consistent?
7. **CTA quality**: are they driving action or just existing?
8. **Competitor gap**: what are peers doing that this brand isn't?
