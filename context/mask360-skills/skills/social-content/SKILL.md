# Mask360 Social Content System

Social content at Mask360 is never generic. Every piece is written for a specific brand voice, a specific audience feeling, and a specific moment in the feed.

---

## Platform-by-Platform Rules

### Instagram (Primary platform for all luxury/premium clients)

**Caption structure:**
- Line 1: Hook: no preamble, no "We are excited", no "Introducing"
- Lines 2-4: Sensory detail, emotion, or brand story, specific, not vague
- Line 5: Quiet punchline or reframe
- CTA: Minimal: location tag, "Link in bio", or nothing at all

**Reel / video text overlays:**
- Max 5 words per card
- Never describe what's on screen, amplify what it means
- Contrast: if visuals are soft, make copy sharp. If dramatic, make copy quiet.

**Carousel copy:**
- Slide 1: Hook (stops the scroll)
- Slides 2-6: One idea per slide, builds tension or revelation
- Last slide: Payoff or CTA, clean, not salesy

### LinkedIn (Mask360 agency channel + select B2B clients)

**Post structure:**
- Line 1: Provocation or POV, make them stop
- Lines 2-5: Story or insight, no buzzwords
- Last line: Implication or question, not a CTA, a thought

**Format:** Prose only. No bullet points unless it's a listicle format. No "Here are 5 things I learned."

## Content Pillar Framework (Client Social Strategy)

When building a content strategy, use these 5 pillars as default:

1. **Product / Hero**: the thing itself, showcased at its best
2. **Story / Heritage**: where it comes from, who made it
3. **Lifestyle / Aspiration**: who the customer is when they use it
4. **Behind the scenes**: process, craft, people
5. **Cultural moment**: festivals, seasons, current events tied to brand values

Each pillar should have a distinct visual and copy treatment.

---

## Influencer / UGC Content Brief Rules

When writing briefs for influencer content:
- Prescribe the feeling, not the script
- Give them 3 approved hooks, not 1 rigid one
- Specify what NOT to say/show
- Include: tone, product placement rules, disclosure format

---

## Things That Never Appear in Mask360 Social Copy

- "We are thrilled to announce"
- "Tag a friend who needs this"
- "Double tap if you agree"
- "Link in bio for more" as the ONLY CTA on every post
- "World class" without proof
- Excessive hashtags (luxury clients: 0-3 max, never in caption body)
- Emoji overload (luxury: none unless brand allows; lifestyle: selective)

---

## Output Format

For a **single post:** Hook + body + CTA + 3 hashtag suggestions (if needed)
For a **content calendar:** Table format: Date / Pillar / Format / Caption / Visual direction
For **reel/video copy:** Frame-by-frame text overlay breakdown + caption
For **carousel:** Slide-by-slide copy

Always deliver primary copy first, then 1-2 variants if needed.
