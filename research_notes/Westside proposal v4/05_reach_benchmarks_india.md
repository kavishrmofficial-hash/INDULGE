# Reach benchmarks for India and the impression model for the three ideas

Prepared for the Mask360 pitch to Westside (Trent Ltd), 16 October 2026. The pitch promises about 15 mn impressions from three ideas: Styled by Westside (target 3.5-5 mn in 12 weeks), the Westside Coffee Cart (target 5-5.5 mn) and Window Stories (target about 4.5 mn). This note collects the benchmarks behind those numbers, builds the model line by line and lists what is still unproven.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. **Status: research incomplete.** No live web research was possible in this pass. The shared WebSearch budget was already used up, and WebFetch failed to resolve any host. Every sourced figure below comes from earlier Mask360 notes (15-16 Oct 2026), and none was re-checked today.
2. Creator **fees by tier** are the only well-sourced input. Per Reel: nano Rs 500-8K, micro Rs 8K-80K (fashion micros with about 50K followers cost Rs 25-60K), macro Rs 84K-7.9 lakh, mega above Rs 2 lakh per collaboration. 3. **Average Reel views and engagement rate by tier in India** were not found. The only proxy is a global vendor figure: about a third of Zara's creators average 1K-5K views (Modash, May 2026).
4. **No sourced figures** were found for meme, clipper, city, college or marketing pages (followers, views, fees), for Meta or YouTube CPMs in India, or for stylist and production rates. The model therefore uses labelled Mask360 assumptions for all of these.
5. Westside's own reach anchors are thin. Instagram had about 1M followers in a stale snippet from about Dec 2024. Trent reported YNG at "6 million views" in Q4 FY26 (company-reported; a second search could not find it). Sharekhan put FY23 reach at about 250K views and engagements a week.

9. **Implied cost:** distribution Rs 46 lakh to Rs 1.72 cr, plus production Rs 25-65 lakh (placeholder rates). This excludes the cart build, window fabrication and Mask360 fees.
10. **Weakest inputs:** views per page post and the Meta CPM. ## 2. Meme, clipper, city, college, marketing and fashion pages

### What is sourced
**Nothing.** No published follower sizes, average views, rate cards or campaign results for Indian meme, clipper, city, college or marketing pages were found in any earlier note. This pass could not search. This is the largest gap in the model, because 39% of the base case comes from these pages.

### How brands use these pages
This is a description of common practice, not a sourced finding.
- **City pages** (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata) run "spotted in the city" or "new in town" posts. Brands buy a post or a collab post and supply the footage, or the page films it. This fits the cart and the windows, which are physical and local.
- **Clipper and meme pages** repost the most shareable 10-15 seconds of someone else's video, usually with a caption hook. Brands seed clips to them on a paid basis. Credit lines vary.
- **Marketing pages** (accounts that break down campaigns for a marketing audience) explain the idea. They reach trade audiences and journalists more than shoppers. Use them for earned PR, not footfall.
- **Fashion pages** (trend, outfit and "get the look" accounts) repost looks. They suit Styled by Westside.
- **College pages** reach campuses. They are relevant only if the cart stops at colleges.
- **Disclosure.** Paid posts on any of these pages need a clear ad label under ASCI's influencer-advertising guidelines (2021). **(unverified this session.)**

### Assumptions used in the model [A]

| Page type | Views per post: low / base / high | Fee per post | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| City page (A-04) | 25K / 50K / 100K | Rs 10K-60K | No anchor. Weakest input. |
| Clipper or meme page (A-05) | 30K / 75K / 150K | Rs 20K-1 lakh | No anchor. Weakest input. |
| Marketing or fashion page (A-06) | 20K / 40K / 80K | Rs 10K-60K | No anchor. |

### How to get real numbers within a week
1. Shortlist 8-10 pages per city and per type.
2. Ask each page for 30-day Insights screenshots: views per Reel, accounts reached, and the audience's city, age and gender split.
3. Use the **median of the last 10 Reels**, not the mean, so one viral post does not inflate the average.
4. Contract a **views floor per post**, with a free repost if the post falls short.
5. Put the median in the model in place of A-04 to A-06.

### What is sourced
**Nothing.** No India CPM for Meta (Instagram or Facebook) or YouTube for 2025-2026 was found in earlier notes, and this pass could not search.

### Assumption used in the model [A]
- Meta Reels placements, metro adults aged about 18-40 interested in fashion: **CPM Rs 200 (low case) / Rs 130 (base) / Rs 80 (high)**, assumption A-07. The low case uses the most expensive CPM.

- **What Rs 1 lakh buys** (arithmetic on A-07): 0.5 mn impressions at Rs 200, 0.77 mn at Rs 130, 1.25 mn at Rs 80. The paid line is the most certain part of the model only if the CPM is right.

### How to make it defensible
- Run the Ads Manager reach and frequency estimate for the real audience (Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru, Reels placements, 12 weeks) and quote that range in the deck.
- If Trent shares Westside's own past CPMs, use those.
- Run creator and page posts as **partnership ads**, so paid spend sits behind the content that already works.
- Report paid impressions from Ads Manager and organic views from post Insights, so no view is counted twice.

## 6. The impression model

### Rules
- **Organic lines:** units × average views per unit = impressions.
- **Paid lines:** budget ÷ CPM × 1,000 = impressions.
- **Counted once:** a collab post between Westside and a creator or page counts once.
- **Excluded from all three cases** (upside, not modelled): earned pickup by unpaid pages, YouTube Shorts and Facebook cross-posts, WhatsApp forwards, store teams' personal accounts, and people who physically see the cart or the windows.
- **High case:** uses only higher averages, never extra units.
- **Units** follow the deck (12 weeks, three cities and 120 looks for Styled) plus Mask360 plan choices, marked [A].

### What the model says
- **Mix of the 14.86 mn base:**

  | Source | Impressions | Share |
  |---|---|---|
  | Paid Meta | 6.15 mn | 41% |
  | Paid page posts (108) | 5.78 mn | 39% |
  | Creator Reels (102 micro, 10 macro) | 1.62 mn | 11% |
  | Westside owned Reels (88) | 1.32 mn | 9% |

  About 80% of the base comes from paid ads and paid page posts. Counting the creator fees too, about 91% is paid for.
- **Effective CPM by channel** (base views, midpoint fees):

  | Channel | Effective CPM |
  |---|---|
  | Paid Meta | Rs 130 |
  | City pages | about Rs 700 |
  | Clipper and meme pages | about Rs 800 |
  | Fashion and marketing pages | about Rs 875 |
  | Macro creators | about Rs 2,500 |
  | Micro creators | about Rs 4,700 |

  Creators cost about 19-36x more per impression than paid. Justify them on the content and the credibility they bring, not on reach.
- **Sensitivity:**
  - If page posts deliver only the low case, the base drops by 3.0 mn.
  - If the CPM is Rs 200 rather than Rs 130, the base drops by 2.2 mn.
  - Both together leave about 9.7 mn.
- **Against the deck labels:** the cart's 5.5 mn is above the modelled base of 5.15 mn. About Rs 46,000 more paid at Rs 130 CPM closes the gap, or change the label to 5 mn. Styled (5.35 mn against 5 mn) and Windows (4.37 mn against 4.5 mn) are close.
- **Against Westside's own baseline:** YNG's reported 6 mn views in Q4 FY26 (R-16, unconfirmed) is the only comparable figure. The plan's 15 mn in 12 weeks would be about 2.5x that quarter. Do not put a "last 12 months" baseline on the slide until Westside shares its Insights.

### Weakest inputs, in order
1. **A-04 and A-05, page views per post.** No source; 39% of the base.
2. **A-07, Meta CPM.** No source; 41% of the base. Fix it with an Ads Manager estimate.
3. **A-01, owned Reel views.** Rests on a stale follower count of about 1M from about Dec 2024.
4. **A-03, macro views.** No source.
5. **A-09, production rates.** Placeholders only.
6. Units per city for city pages. Six posts per city per idea may exhaust each city's few large pages, and repeat posts on the same page get fewer views. This matters most for the smaller cities in Window Stories (Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune).

### How to present 15 mn defensibly
- **Option A: guaranteed with a make-good.** Present 15 mn as the plan and hold a **paid make-good reserve of about Rs 13.5 lakh**. That covers the low-case shortfall of 6.76 mn at Rs 200 CPM, or Rs 8.8 lakh at Rs 130. Release it only if weekly tracking falls behind.
- **Option B: floor and plan.** Commit to a floor of about 8-10 mn and plan for 15 mn.
- **Either way:** contract a views floor with every paid page, and report paid and organic separately each week.
