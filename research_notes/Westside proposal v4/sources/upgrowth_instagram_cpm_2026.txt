 Instagram Ads prices in India have shifted hard in the last 18 months. Advantage+ placements are now Meta’s default setting, so budgets spread across Reels, Stories, Feed and Explore unless you narrow them. Yet most founders we talk to still quote CPM numbers from 2022 playbooks, which is why their agencies look expensive and their ROAS looks broken.

 This Instagram Ads pricing India guide for 2026 covers cost per impression, click, view and day, Reels vs Stories vs Feed, boosting, agency fees and monthly budgets. At upGrowth Digital , we manage Instagram budgets for D2C beauty, fashion and F&B brands, and our view is simple: Instagram still works, but only if you respect what the auction has become.

 Budget alone rarely fixes paid social. When upGrowth restructured Lendingkart’s paid acquisition, lead volume grew 5.7x and cost per lead fell 30%. The same principle holds on Instagram: fix accountability, creative and measurement before you add spend.

 This page is Instagram-only: placements, boosted posts and per-view pricing. For Facebook placements, see our Facebook ads cost guide for India .

 Instagram Ads Pricing India 2026: What You Actually Pay 

 Instagram ads cost in India runs Rs 45 to Rs 400 CPM and Rs 4 to Rs 55 CPC in 2026 for most prospecting campaigns, with retargeting audiences reaching Rs 800 CPM. A working performance program needs Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 1.5 lakh a month in media, or Rs 1.65 lakh to Rs 2.3 lakh all-in. 

 Every range here is an upGrowth estimate, September 2026, based on campaigns we manage. There’s no rate card: an auction sets each price , weighing your bid strategy, bid amount and the probability of getting your chosen result. So a fashion brand targeting metro women in wedding season can pay 2 to 3 times what a regional F&B brand pays in February.

 CPM by audience temperature

 Cold prospecting to a broad lookalike costs Rs 80 to Rs 180 CPM. Warm retargeting to engaged users costs Rs 250 to Rs 500 because you’re bidding for a smaller pool. Cart abandoners run Rs 400 to Rs 800 but often still convert profitably.

 Instagram ad price in India: why a low CPM isn’t a low CPC

 A cheap impression on low-quality inventory rarely drives a click. Most D2C categories pay Rs 8 to Rs 25 per click, while beauty and skincare hit Rs 15 to Rs 40 because the auction is dense. Regional language creative in tier 2 cities often drops CPC to Rs 4 to Rs 12 without hurting conversion rate.

 Instagram price per view in India

 Video campaigns cost Rs 25 to Rs 180 per 1,000 views, about 2.5 paise to 18 paise per view. Meta’s billing events include impressions, link clicks and ThruPlay, so check what a quote is billed on before you compare.

 Instagram Ads Pricing India by Category: CPM and CPC Benchmarks 

 Instagram CPM in India ranges from Rs 90 to Rs 220 for F&B up to Rs 200 to Rs 400 for fintech, and CPC from Rs 6 to Rs 18 up to Rs 20 to Rs 55. Averages lie, so benchmark Instagram Ads pricing India data against your vertical and funnel stage.

 Beauty and personal care

 CPM: Rs 180 to Rs 320. CPC: Rs 15 to Rs 40. CPL for WhatsApp leads: Rs 120 to Rs 280. Cost per purchase: Rs 450 to Rs 1,200. Reels often beats Feed on CPA here, but at high volume it needs a refresh every 7 to 10 days because fatigue is brutal.

 Fashion and apparel

 CPM: Rs 140 to Rs 280. CPC: Rs 12 to Rs 35. Cost per add to cart: Rs 60 to Rs 160. Cost per purchase: Rs 380 to Rs 950. Carousels still tend to beat single-video Reels for catalog-heavy brands.

 F&B and quick commerce

 CPM: Rs 90 to Rs 220. CPC: Rs 6 to Rs 18. Cost per first order: Rs 180 to Rs 450 with a promo code. Awareness builds can get cost per 1,000 video views down to Rs 40 to Rs 80, which makes Reels the default format.

 Fintech

 CPM: Rs 200 to Rs 400. CPC: Rs 20 to Rs 55. CPL to a qualified form: Rs 280 to Rs 750. BFSI advertisers bid hard, and claims-language rules force extra creative variants.

 EdTech

 CPM: Rs 160 to Rs 340. CPC: Rs 14 to Rs 38. CPL: Rs 200 to Rs 600. Parent-targeting runs a hotter CPM than student-targeting because deeper-pocketed brands dominate it.

 D2C electronics and home

 CPM: Rs 120 to Rs 250. CPC: Rs 10 to Rs 28. Cost per purchase: Rs 500 to Rs 1,400. AOV matters more than CPA, because these categories often turn profitable only at 2.5x to 3x ROAS.

 The lowest cost per outcome goes to brands with the highest creative throughput, not the cheapest CPM. Shipping 3 to 5 new Reels a week beats optimizing bids on 2-year-old creative.

 Instagram Ad Formats and Costs: Reels vs Stories vs Feed vs Explore 

 Reels is the cheapest Instagram placement at Rs 45 to Rs 140 CPM, less than half of Feed at Rs 150 to Rs 350, with Explore (Rs 60 to Rs 160) and Stories (Rs 80 to Rs 200) in between. 

 Meta’s Marketing API lists 8 Instagram placements , including feed, profile feed, Stories, Reels, Explore and search results. After creative cadence, placement mix is the biggest lever in Instagram Ads pricing India decisions.

 Reels ads

 Reels offers the cheapest CPM for most D2C categories, but only with native vertical video. Horizontal footage cropped to vertical looks like an ad and gets skipped. Minimum viable setup: 3 Reels ad variants tested per week.

 Stories ads

 Stories are full-screen and tap-driven, a strong fit for retargeting and promo-heavy creative. Build for the vertical frame instead of reusing Feed assets.

 Feed ads

 Feed is the most expensive placement, but in our experience it reaches the highest-intent audience. It suits catalog ads, carousels and brands with an established organic presence.

 Explore ads

 Explore is cheap per impression, second only to Reels, but conversion intent is lowest because people there are browsing, not shopping. Use it for awareness, not performance.

 Advantage+ sales campaigns

 Meta’s Advantage+ sales campaigns replace Advantage+ shopping campaigns, and Meta picks placements, so CPMs blend. They suit brands with 50+ SKUs and strong catalog data, and they’re a poor fit for brands still finding product-market fit.

 A starting placement mix

 For most D2C brands starting fresh, allocate 50% to 60% to Reels, 15% to 20% to Stories, 15% to 20% to Feed and 5% to 10% to experiments (Explore, Advantage+). Rebalance monthly on actual cost per result.

 Instagram Boost Post Price in India: What Boosting Costs 

 Boosting a post or Reel has no fixed price in India. You set a daily budget and duration, the post runs as an ad, and in our experience it lands in the same CPM range as its placement, such as Rs 45 to Rs 140 for Reels. 

 How boosting works

 Per Instagram’s guide to boosting a post with Ad tools , you need a professional account. You pick a post, a goal (profile visits, website visits or messages), an audience, a daily budget and a duration, and the ad runs after review. Posts with product tags can be boosted too.

 Boost cost per day and the Apple fee

 You choose the daily spend. Rs 500 a day is our working floor for a test, or Rs 15,000 over 30 days. Instagram also notes that funds added through its iOS app are charged via the Apple App Store, which keeps a service fee, so add funds another way.

 When to move to Ads Manager

 Need purchase goals, retargeting or Conversions API tracking? Move to Ads Manager. Looking for an Instagram post price list? That’s usually influencer rates, which creators set. Plan them with our influencer marketing budget calculator .

 Instagram Ads Agency Management Fees in India 

 Agency fees for managing Instagram ads in India run from Rs 15,000 a month for a freelancer to Rs 10 lakh+ for an enterprise agency, across 4 tiers. These are market ranges (upGrowth estimate, September 2026), so choose by how much work you want in-house.

 Freelancer: Rs 15,000 to Rs 45,000 a month

 A solo operator runs your media buy while you supply the Reels. It fits spend under Rs 3 lakh a month. The wrong move is a Rs 45,000 freelancer running Rs 15 lakh of monthly spend: a 3% fee on a budget where errors compound fast.

 Boutique agency: Rs 50,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh a month

 A 2 to 4 person team covers media buying, creative strategy and some production, with weekly reporting. It fits brands spending Rs 3 lakh to Rs 10 lakh a month that need creative support.

 Full-scope performance agency: Rs 1.5 lakh to Rs 4 lakh a month

 A 5 to 8 person pod adds production, an analyst, Conversions API setup, attribution and a monthly shoot. It earns its fee only at Rs 10 lakh+ a month in spend or when Instagram is your primary paid channel.

 Enterprise agency: Rs 4 lakh to Rs 10 lakh+ a month

 This tier outsources your whole marketing function and fits brands spending Rs 25 lakh+ a month across several channels.

 Retainer, percentage of spend and creative costs

 Most D2C brands pay a flat retainer. A percentage-of-spend fee (usually 10% to 15%, with a floor) is common above Rs 15 lakh a month. Charging both is double billing. Creative is usually extra: Rs 8,000 to Rs 15,000 per boutique shoot day, Rs 25,000 to Rs 60,000 for full production, or Rs 40,000 to Rs 2 lakh a month for a package. Compare options in our guide to a Meta ads agency vs a freelancer vs in-house .

 Instagram Advertising Cost India Per Month and Per Day: Budget Planning 

 Plan Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 1.5 lakh a month in media, about Rs 4,000 to Rs 5,000 a day, and Rs 1.65 lakh to Rs 2.3 lakh a month all-in with creative and management. 

 Below Rs 1 lakh a month in media, most ad sets can’t generate enough conversions for Meta’s delivery system to finish learning, so CPAs stay volatile. That floor is the most overlooked part of Instagram Ads pricing India planning.

 Instagram advertising cost per month: the breakdown

 Media, Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 1.5 lakh: 60% Reels, 20% Stories, 15% Feed, 5% Explore or Advantage+, across 3 campaigns (cold prospecting, warm retargeting, cart retargeting) with separate creative per stage.

 Creative, Rs 30,000 to Rs 50,000: at least 12 Reels a month, 8 to 10 static assets and fresh hooks every 14 to 21 days.

 Management, Rs 15,000 to Rs 30,000: a freelancer or consultant, or an opportunity cost if you run it in-house. A Rs 30,000 to Rs 50,000 monthly budget buys a test, not a program.

 Instagram advertising cost per day in India

 There’s no fixed daily fee. For conversion campaigns, give each prospecting ad set Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000 a day and let retargeting share the rest of a Rs 4,000 to Rs 5,000 daily budget. Awareness or traffic tests can start at Rs 500 a day per ad set.

 Minimum budget and GST

 Meta sets a minimum budget for each ad set based on the billing event and converts non-USD budgets at setup, so Ads Manager shows your rupee floor. Treat tax as a separate line: Meta’s Business Help Center covers India’s Goods and Services Tax for ads, so check your billing summary before you lock a number.

 Channel share and CPA targets

 Keep Instagram at 20% to 35% of total paid media; above 40% leaves you exposed to auction shifts. At scale, aim for CPA at 25% to 40% of AOV on first orders and 12% to 20% on repeat purchases. Our guide to improving ROAS for D2C brands in India covers the levers.

 3 checks before you approve an Instagram budget

 Is creative funded separately from media? If both come from the same bucket, you’re underpaying for both.

 Is the Conversions API running with Event Match Quality at 8 or higher? Meta scores EMQ out of 10 and recommends sending the same events through the pixel and the Conversions API .

 Does someone own refresh cadence? Refresh high-volume campaigns (Rs 1 lakh+ a month each) every 7 to 10 days and lower-volume campaigns every 14 to 21 days.

 If you can’t say yes to all 3, fix those before adding spend.

 Instagram Ads Pricing India: Frequently Asked Questions 

 How much do Instagram ads cost in India in 2026?

 Instagram ads cost in India typically runs Rs 45 to Rs 400 per 1,000 impressions (CPM) and Rs 4 to Rs 55 per click (CPC) for prospecting, with retargeting audiences reaching Rs 800 CPM (upGrowth estimate, September 2026). Reels is the cheapest placement at Rs 45 to Rs 140 CPM and Feed the priciest at Rs 150 to Rs 350, because an auction, not a rate card, sets the price.

 What is the Instagram advertising cost in India per day?

 There’s no fixed daily charge. You set the budget above Meta’s minimum, which depends on the billing event and shows in rupees in Ads Manager. For performance campaigns, plan Rs 1,500 to Rs 3,000 a day per prospecting ad set and about Rs 4,000 to Rs 5,000 a day in total. Awareness or traffic tests can start at Rs 500 a day.

 How much does Instagram advertising cost per month in India?

 A performance program on Instagram needs Rs 1.2 lakh to Rs 1.5 lakh a month in media, or Rs 1.65 lakh to Rs 2.3 lakh all-in once you add Rs 30,000 to Rs 50,000 of creative and Rs 15,000 to Rs 30,000 of management (upGrowth estimate, September 2026). A Rs 30,000 to Rs 50,000 monthly budget buys a test, not a program.

 Are Reels ads cheaper than Feed ads on Instagram in India?

 Yes. Reels CPM runs Rs 45 to Rs 140 for most D2C categories in 2026, less than half of Feed at Rs 150 to Rs 350 (upGrowth estimate, September 2026). Explore sits at Rs 60 to Rs 160 and Stories at Rs 80 to Rs 200. Reels only stays cheap with native vertical video, because cropped horizontal footage pushes cost per result up.

 What is the Instagram boost post price in India?

 Boosting has no fixed price. You pick a goal, audience, daily budget and duration in the Instagram app, and the post runs as an ad after review. In our experience, costs track the matching placement, such as Rs 45 to Rs 140 CPM for boosted Reels. Rs 500 a day is a sensible floor for a test, and funds added through the iOS app carry an Apple service fee.

 What is a good ROAS for Instagram ads for D2C brands in India?

 Target a blended ROAS of 2.5x to 3.5x if you’re a mid-stage D2C brand spending Rs 2 lakh to Rs 15 lakh a month. Cold prospecting often runs at 1.5x to 2.2x, below break-even on the first purchase, and earns back through retargeting at 4x to 6x. Judge prospecting and retargeting together, not campaign by campaign.

 Your Next Move: Audit Your Instagram Ads Economics Before You Scale 

 Before you add budget, find where the current budget leaks. Most D2C Instagram accounts we audit lose money to 3 fixable issues: broken Conversions API tracking, refresh cycles longer than 21 days and over-allocation to Feed. None needs more money, just someone owning the diagnostics.

 If you spend Rs 2 lakh+ a month without knowing true CPA by placement and audience stage, that’s a measurement problem, and it’s what our paid media team checks first. Instagram Ads pricing India benchmarks show what others pay. Your own CPA by placement shows what to do next.
