 Westside Introduces FRONT ROW with 80 Seasonal Looks and New Talent Initiatives

 BY -
 Licence India Bureau 

 Oct, 03 2026
 /

 Fashion and lifestyle retailer Westside has launched FRONT ROW, a new fashion and culture platform designed to bring together style, creativity and emerging talent. The initiative expands beyond a traditional fashion showcase, positioning fashion as a medium for self-expression, discovery and collaboration with new creative voices. 
 The debut edition of FRONT ROW featured 80 curated Autumn/Winter/Festive looks, offering a preview of Westside’s fashion direction for the upcoming season. The event also marked the unveiling of the brand’s second YNG designer launch and the latest W Edit collection, bringing together contemporary fashion and fresh design perspectives.
 The platform brought together 150 micro and macro culture-shapers from the fashion and creative industries. Moving away from the conventional runway format, FRONT ROW created an immersive experience where designers, models, dancers, and content creators interacted within Westside’s evolving creative ecosystem.
 A key component of the initiative is its focus on nurturing emerging talent across fashion and culture. As part of the platform, Westside introduced Tuxedo Street, its first Dance Hall x Boiler Room-inspired concept, combining music, movement and fashion through a competitive dance-off format. Building on this initiative, the retailer plans to launch three national Dance Off challenges, providing aspiring dancers with opportunities to showcase their skills and gain wider recognition.
 Westside also announced 30 New Faces for 2026/2027, aimed at supporting emerging talent within the modelling industry. Over the next year, these models will receive mentorship and participate in the brand’s campaigns, digital content and runway presentations, helping them gain industry exposure and practical experience.
 Umashan Naidoo, Chief Creative, Brand & Beauty, Westside said , “FRONT ROW is not just a fashion show. It is a platform that discovers, encourages and creates a safe space for emerging talent to be launched. We are celebrating creativity, championing young visionaries and building our YNG community by giving them front row access to all our big ideas.”
 Through initiatives including the seasonal fashion showcase, YNG designer launch, W Edit collection, Tuxedo Street, the 30 New Faces programme and participation from 150 culture-shapers, FRONT ROW brings together Westside’s fashion and creative efforts under a single platform. The initiative reflects the retailer’s focus on supporting contemporary fashion and creating opportunities for the next generation of designers, models and creative professionals in India.

 Aretto Launches New Kids’ Footwear Ranges With ‘Free the Child’ Campaign

 BY - Licence India Bureau 

 Oct, 15 2026 
 / 4 MIN READ 

 Kids’ footwear brand Aretto has introduced its new campaign, ‘U/A: Free the Child’, focusing on children’s curiosity, individuality and freedom to explore. The campaign also marks the introduction of two footwear ranges, Leaps Pro V and Leaps Pro, developed for children aged 8 to 13 years. 
 The campaign challenges conventional perceptions of growing up, encouraging children to retain their curiosity, express their individuality and question everyday norms. Through the perspective of Generation Alpha and tweens, the initiative highlights the importance of allowing children to explore the world independently and develop their own identities.
 The campaign’s ‘U/A’ concept draws inspiration from film certification terminology, using it to represent the unrestricted spirit of childhood. It also encourages adults to reconsider expectations and limitations that may affect how children express themselves as they grow older.
 Satyajit Mittal, Founder and CEO, Aretto said , “Growing up should open up a child’s world, not narrow it. The ages of 8 to 13 are discovering their individuality and learning to question the ordinary, and there is much adults can learn from their curiosity. ‘Free the Child’ celebrates that spirit and reminds us that growing up need not mean growing out of who we are. This belief also shapes how we design footwear. As children grow older, their activities, movements and footwear needs evolve. With Leaps Pro V and Leaps Pro, we bring together the support older kids need with the adaptive technology, breathability and flexibility that define Aretto.”
 The campaign also reflects Aretto’s approach to designing footwear that accommodates children’s changing physical needs. As children move between school, sports and recreational activities, their footwear requirements evolve, making support, flexibility and durability important considerations.
 The ranges feature flexible, barefoot-style soles intended to support natural movement. Their non-marking outsoles also make them suitable for school corridors and indoor sports courts. 
 Additionally, the Pro range includes midfoot panels and a padded collar designed to help secure the foot and heel during quick, multidirectional movements. Leaps Pro V comes with Velcro straps, while Leaps Pro features elastic laces that can be tied once and worn like slip-ons.

 Greenply Industries Limited has launched XPro, a new hybrid veneer range under its Woodcrest brand , designed for contemporary residential and corporate interiors. The range combines the natural warmth and texture of wood with consistent, repeatable patterns, enabling architects, interior designers and homeowners to create coordinated interiors across multiple surfaces. 
 The launch comes amid steady growth in India’s home interiors market, estimated to be expanding at 8–13% annually. Rising demand from integrated townships and premium residential projects is driving greater investment in interior design, with premium households spending nearly 10–20% of their property value on interiors. 
 The growing preference for “quiet luxury” and understated, premium aesthetics is also influencing interior design choices. Consumers are increasingly opting for natural materials, refined finishes and products that offer durability alongside visual appeal. Greater digital access to design inspiration has further encouraged homeowners to explore premium options and choose established brands that offer consistent quality and reliability. 
 At the same time, large residential developments and corporate projects require uniform finishes across different spaces and applications. From wardrobes and doors to furniture and wall panelling, maintaining a consistent design language has become an important consideration for architects and interior designers. 
 XPro has been developed to address these requirements by combining the character of natural wood with reproducible designs that can be used across multiple surfaces. Its range of patterns caters to different interior styles, making it easier for homeowners to achieve a coordinated look while helping design professionals execute a consistent vision across residential and corporate projects. 
 Commenting on the launch, Sanidhya Mittal, Joint Managing Director, Greenply Industries Limited, said, “We are seeing a clear shift in how premium homes in India are being designed. Homeowners are investing a larger share of their property value in interiors, and veneer has become one of the defining choices in that spend. At the same time, the growth of integrated townships and premium residential projects means designers need a finish that stays consistent across every unit and every surface. Buyers are also more informed than ever, discovering designs online and choosing brands they can trust for quality and longevity. XPro has been developed for this customer, offering the richness of natural wood with the consistency today’s projects demand, backed by Greenply’s commitment to design and durability.” 
 The XPro range is suitable for various residential applications, including wardrobes, furniture, doors, feature walls and wall panelling. It can also be used in corporate environments such as reception areas and workspaces. Positioned in the mid-premium segment , the range targets projects where design, durability and aesthetic consistency are key considerations.

The new range is aimed at strengthening the company’s offering for architects, interior designers and dealers seeking wood-inspired solutions for contemporary spaces.

 "The best collaborations force you to see something familiar through an entirely new lens. Rid has a very distinct eye for Mumbai, its light, architecture, colours, people, and contradictions.
