 Trent Share Price Gains Over 11% as Q2 FY27 Revenue Jumps 23% to ₹5,788 Crore
 Written by: Team Angel One Updated on: 7 Oct 2026, 9:51 pm IST 
 Trent's Q2 FY27 revenue grew by 23% to ₹5,788 crore, with 1,342 stores operational, including the 1,000th Zudio store. 

 Share 
 On October 5, 2026, Trent Limited announced its revenue and store count update for Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27, as per the exchange filings.    
 The company reported a 23% year-on-year growth in standalone revenue from operations, reaching ₹5,788 crore in Q2 FY27.    
 Additionally, Trent expanded its retail footprint by opening its 1,000th Zudio store, bringing the total number of stores to 1,342 across various brands.   
 Trent's Revenue Performance in Q2 FY27   
 During Q2 FY27, Trent's standalone revenue from operations, excluding GST, reached ₹5,788 crore, marking a 23% increase compared to ₹4,724 crore in Q2 FY26.    
 The revenue from merchandise sales also grew by 23% during this period. For the first half of FY27, the revenue stood at ₹11,454 crore, a 21% rise from ₹9,505 crore in H1 FY26.

 Trent's Store Expansion and Milestones   
 Trent achieved a significant milestone by opening its 1,000th Zudio store during the quarter. As of September 30, 2026, the company operates 1,342 stores across its Westside, Zudio, and other lifestyle brands.    
 In Q2 FY27, Trent opened 10 Westside stores and 17 Zudio stores. For the first half of FY27, the company opened 11 Westside stores and 36 Zudio stores.

 Audit and Reporting   
 The standalone revenue figures provided are subject to audit by the company's statutory auditors. A detailed press release will be available on Trent's official website for further information.   
 Trent Share Price Performance   
 As of October 06, 2026, at 9:57 AM, Trent share price on NSE was trading at ₹2,867.20, up by 11.13% from the previous closing price.   
 Conclusion   
 Trent's Q2 FY27 revenue increased by 23% to ₹5,788 crore, with H1 FY27 revenue rising by 21% to ₹11,454 crore. The company expanded its store network, reaching 1,342 stores, including the 1,000th Zudio store.
