# How Westside positioned its communication, mid-October 2025 to 16 October 2026

Prepared for the Mask360 pitch to Westside (Trent Ltd). Window: 16 Oct 2025 to 16 Oct 2026. Indian FY: FY26 = Apr 2025 to Mar 2026; Q1 FY27 = Apr to Jun 2026; Q2 FY27 = Jul to Sep 2026.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. Westside communicated mainly through platforms it owns, not campaigns: Wesness (women's running), YNG (young creators), FRONT ROW (fashion and culture, launched 1 Oct 2026) and one music collaboration (W.i.S.H.).
2. We found no named brand film, umbrella tagline, celebrity ambassador or creative-agency credit in the window. The last agency-credited brand line we found is "Endless Possibilities" (Ogilvy, about 2011).
3. Festive and occasion moments appeared as product edits on westside.com: a burnt-orange Diwali 2025 guide, a Kala by E.T.A wedding guide and the "9 Days 9 Colours" Navratri 2026 edit. We found no Diwali 2025, end-of-season sale, summer, monsoon, Independence Day, Durga Puja or Diwali 2026 campaign.
4. The busiest stretch was February to July 2026, with roughly one Wesness event a month. The biggest was the 8 Feb Mumbai Fun Run: 3,500+ women per Trent's filing, 3,600+ in the later deck. We found no Wesness event after 19 Jul 2026.
5. Five themes recur: new Indian talent; culture beyond clothes (music, dance, fitness); a women's community; Westside's own labels; and fashion credibility through seasonal showcases.
6. The tone has two registers. Everyday content is warm and inclusive ("celebrates imperfections", "a safe space … without judgement"). FRONT ROW is editorial and runway-led.
7. Two audiences are addressed: the core woman shopper (Wesness, ethnic edits) and young creatives (YNG, FRONT ROW, W.i.S.H.). Men, kids and home were barely visible in what we found.
8. Investor channels also serve as brand channels. Trent filed the Wesness press release with the stock exchanges, and its decks report YNG and Wesness results. Those results are counted in sign-ups, views and shares; no published result we found is a store-visit figure.
9. The strongest assets are self-funding communities that start at stores, a talent pipeline that also produces content, 11.85 mn active WestStyleClub members (FY26), and a network that grew from 261 to 311 stores in the year to 30 Sep 2026.
10. Limits: this pass could not run new searches or open any page (the shared search budget was used up and every host failed to resolve). Every item comes from the 15-16 Oct 2026 notes, which were built from search-result snippets, and each one is tagged for re-checking.

## 1. Month-by-month record (what Westside said and showed)

Context just before the window: YNG launched at Westside's Media Day on 11 Sep 2025. NIFT designers reworked Bombay Paisley and E.T.A into a Gen Z capsule, filmed as the mini-series "From Sketch to Store" (P-66, P-67). The Q2 FY26 business update came out on 6 Oct 2025 (P-44).

**October 2025 (16-31): Diwali 2025, around 20 Oct**
- Festive work appeared as owned web content: a burnt-orange "trend colour" style guide for the A/W'25 festive drop, and an "Ethnic Wear for Diwali 2025" collection page. Publish dates are not confirmed (P-01).
- We found no Diwali 2025 film or named campaign. Westside does not appear in Campaign Asia's Diwali 2025 roundup (P-02).
- Shoppers and creators filled the gap with store haul videos, such as "Westside Latest Diwali Collection 2025 | Westside New arrivals | Westside Starting at 199/-" (P-03).
- Business-press framing from around this time: Westside "targets GenZ customers with portfolio revamp" (ICICI Direct news note quoting the Westside COO) (P-04).

**November 2025: wedding season**
- A westside.com style guide, "Trending Wedding Outfits for Men 2025", built on KALA by E.T.A bandhgalas and pastel kurtas. The date is inferred, and this is SEO content rather than a campaign (P-05).
- The Q2 FY26 investor presentation described YNG as created "to celebrate and nurture India's next generation of fashion creators". It came out after 6 Oct 2025; the exact date was not captured (P-06).

**December 2025: party season**
- **Westside × W.i.S.H. "Glitz & Glam"**: a sequinned, stage-styled party capsule with the Indian I-pop girl group W.i.S.H., sold in store and online. The launch date is estimated at about late Dec 2025 from page-age metadata (P-09, P-10).

## 2. The record by type

**Campaigns, lines and brand films**
- No umbrella campaign, tagline or brand film was found in the window (P-02, P-35, P-46). The last agency brand line we found is "Endless Possibilities" (Ogilvy, with Giselli Monteiro, about 2011) (P-53).
- The names Westside did use are platform and collection names, not brand lines: "Wesness", "YNG (Young New Game-changer)", "FRONT ROW", "Tuxedo Street", "30 New Faces", "W Edit", "Glitz & Glam", "9 Days 9 Colours", "Run2Rave / Run2Quiz / Run2Zumba / Run2Stepup", and the content line "Run, Style, Repeat" (P-68).
- Creative is described as in-house. Naidoo's undated interview line is "Everything we do has to be analyzed & has to have a return on investment" (P-52).
- How others describe the brand:
  - Tata CLiQ's app listing calls Westside "the trusted high-street favourite" (P-54).
  - Trent's FY26 BRSR calls it Trent's "leading lifestyle concept" (P-55).
  - Bernstein says it could become "the benchmark department store format in India" (P-56).

**Festive and seasonal work**
- Diwali 2025 got a style guide and a collection page (P-01). Wedding season got a men's style guide (P-05). Navratri 2026 got the "9 Days 9 Colours" edit (P-36).
- We found nothing for the end-of-season sale or Republic Day, summer, monsoon, Independence Day, Durga Puja, or Diwali 2026 so far (P-11, P-35, P-46).
- The festive unit is the edit page, not a story.

**Collaborations**
- W.i.S.H. (I-pop girl group, launched with Sony Music India) was used twice: the Glitz & Glam capsule (about Dec 2025) and a live set at Wesness Mumbai (Feb 2026) (P-09, P-10, P-12).
- FRONT ROW featured a YNG designer launch, and it is the platform for New Faces (P-38, P-39).
- No designer or celebrity capsule was found for 2026 (P-09).

**Communities**
- **Wesness**: about 25,000 women in four cities (P-13). Its formats are women's fun runs and "Run2" hybrids at Rs 399-699 (P-16 to P-20). Several start at Westside stores (P-16, P-17, P-18). Results are reported only as registrations and participants (P-12, P-14).
- **YNG**: an open community for designers, stylists, models, photographers, videographers, influencers and make-up artists, described as "a community powered by creativity" (P-67). It is reported as a content-sourcing engine (P-22), and its engagement figures are unconfirmed (P-23).
- **WestStyleClub** (loyalty, Rs 199 a year) is the largest relationship asset: 11.85 mn active members in FY26 (P-60, P-61, P-62). We found no communication that links it to Wesness, YNG or FRONT ROW.

**Faces**
- No celebrity ambassador was found.
- The faces in the window were W.i.S.H. (music), YNG designers and the coming 30 New Faces (in-house talent), plus two executives: Shailina Parti (business press, conclaves) and Umashan Naidoo (FRONT ROW) (P-09, P-38, P-28, P-41, P-42).
- Older "Westside People" YouTube videos give both executives a voice. Naidoo: "A store that is a safe space, where a customer can experiment without judgement." Parti: "Our job in fashion is to make strategic bets, and get it right." Both are undated (P-47, P-48).

**In-store events and store-launch communication**
- Wesness used stores as start points and venues (P-16 to P-18). FRONT ROW was an event, not an in-store programme (P-38).
- Openings were steady (261 to 311, +50 net in the year to 30 Sep 2026; 60 opened in FY26, "the highest in our history") (P-43, P-44, P-33), but we found almost no public launch communication (P-70).
- Trent used numbered-store press releases before the window (the 243rd and 244th stores, early 2025). None was found inside it (P-70).

**Corporate voice used as brand voice**
- Trent filed the Wesness press release with BSE (P-12).
- Investor decks carry the YNG and Wesness metrics (P-22, P-14).
- The CEO's biggest public moment was a growth interview with Bloomberg (P-28).
- This builds credibility with investors and trade, but it reaches few shoppers.

## 3. Positioning analysis

**Recurring themes**
1. *A stage for new Indian talent*: YNG, the second YNG designer, 30 New Faces, and FRONT ROW's "emerging talent" (P-06, P-22, P-38, P-39).
2. *Culture beyond clothes*: I-pop music (W.i.S.H.), DJ sets, dance-offs (Tuxedo Street), fitness formats (Zumba, HIIT, quiz runs) (P-09, P-12, P-17, P-18, P-20, P-38).
3. *Women together*: women-only runs, a 25,000-strong community, and a CEO who appears at women's leadership forums (P-12, P-13, P-42).
4. *Own labels as the hero*: Bombay Paisley, E.T.A, Kala by E.T.A, Utsa, Vark, W Edit; YNG reworks own labels rather than outside brands (P-05, P-36, P-38, P-66).
5. *Fashion credibility on a seasonal calendar*: a Media Day in Sep 2025, then FRONT ROW in Oct 2026, each showing the next season (P-66, P-38).

**Tone**
- Everyday tone (agency and executive descriptions): warm, inclusive, imperfect. The quoted phrases are "celebrates imperfections and supports growth, learning and real conversations" and "a safe space, where a customer can experiment without judgement". A topical-day character, "Hi Cutie", is also mentioned (P-49, P-50, P-47).
- Event tone: editorial and youth-cultural (runway looks, Boiler Room-style dance, I-pop) (P-38).
- Corporate tone: numbers-led and growth-led (stores, AI design speed, online share) (P-28, P-30).
- These three registers are not joined by a shared line or a visual device that we could find. A feed review is needed to confirm this.

**Who it speaks to**
- Core: urban women, who are the Wesness audience and buy the ethnic and festive edits. Older sources describe Westside as a family store whose most loyal shoppers are women (P-13, P-36; see the business note, section 6).
- Growth: Gen Z and young creatives (YNG, FRONT ROW, W.i.S.H.), in line with Parti's "44 per cent growth in Gen Z customers" and the youth labels growing about 30% (P-32).
- Trade and investors: the filings, decks and Bloomberg.
- Under-addressed in what we found: men (one wedding guide), kids, home and beauty (Studiowest blog posts only), even though Westside sells all of them and the YouTube channel lists fashion, food, beauty and home (P-05, P-15, P-59). Kids and home marketing were not searched in depth, so treat this as a search gap.

**What the brand promise appears to be** (our inference; Westside has published no promise statement that we found)
- "Trusted, own-label fashion for the whole wardrobe, with no discount cycles, now also a place where new Indian talent and women's community happen."
- Support: "trusted high-street favourite" (P-54); about 100% private label (01 note, W-44); avoiding discounts (P-11); the YNG, FRONT ROW and Wesness framing (P-22, P-38, P-13).
- The promise is visible in actions more than in words. No line states it.

**Channels that carry it**
- Owned web: westside.com blog and collection pages (P-01, P-05, P-07, P-09, P-36).
- Owned social: @westsidestores on Instagram, YouTube, X, Pinterest and Threads. No current audience figures; the latest snippet (about Dec 2024) shows about 1M Instagram followers and 83 posts (P-57, P-58).
- Events, with registration listed on running-event platforms (indiarunning, RaceMart, Endorfin) (P-16 to P-20).
- Trent exchange filings, decks and the AGM (P-12, P-22, P-34).
- Trade and business press: Fashion Value Chain, t2online, Indian Retailer, exchange4media, Bloomberg, BW Businessworld (P-38, P-26, P-28, P-32).
- Commerce surfaces: Tata Neu, Tata CLiQ Fashion and global.westside.com (GCC) (P-54, P-71).
- Not found: TV, outdoor, print advertising, an ambassador, or any trade coverage of paid social or a media mandate.

**What is consistent**
- Owned platforms over bought media; own labels over outside brands; no discount messaging; the store used as a venue (Wesness); a seasonal showcase each autumn; investor-grade reporting of community metrics.

**What is missing or thin** (absence findings; confirm with a feed review)
- An umbrella line that ties the platforms together (P-53).
- A festive story across Navratri, Durga Puja, Diwali and the wedding season, rather than edit pages (P-02, P-46).
- Communication around store openings, during a year of +50 net stores (P-70, P-43).
- Any public link from community or creator activity to store visits or WestStyleClub (P-12, P-22).
- A continuous Wesness calendar after July 2026, and any Wesness presence in Delhi or the north-east, the stated growth regions (P-21, P-31).
- Public cities and dates for the FRONT ROW Dance Off tour (P-38).
- Men's, kids', home and beauty storytelling at the level given to women's and Gen Z.
- Communication of product freshness to shoppers, even though the design engine is fast (P-30).

**Where it is strong**
- Communities that pay for themselves and start at stores. Wesness charges Rs 399-699 entry, and the largest run drew 3,500+ women (P-12, P-16 to P-20).
- A talent pipeline that doubles as a content supply ("strengthening Westside's content sourcing") (P-22).
- Culture moments that suit Gen Z (dance-offs, I-pop) at a time when Gen Z customers are reported up 44% (P-32, P-38).
- A loyalty base of 11.85 mn active members, with members accounting for over 90% of revenue in FY25 (P-60, P-61).
- Credibility with the business press, plus a first-time Kantar BrandZ India entry (No. 38, unverified release date) (P-08, P-56).

## 4. Opportunities

Each item is tied to the claims above. None uses another brand as a model.

1. **Westside already starts Wesness runs at its stores** (Gachibowli, New Commercial Street, Sky Vista DN Nagar II) and charges Rs 399-699 entry. **It could end each event in the store**, with kit pick-up, a fitting or WestStyleClub sign-up, and report store visits next to turnout. **Why:** Trent reports Wesness only as registrations and participants, while fashion like-for-like growth is in the low single digits (P-12, P-14, P-16 to P-18, P-64).
2. **Westside already ran a Wesness event almost every month from February to July 2026 in four cities.** **It could keep a published, year-round calendar and take Wesness formats to its growth regions.** **Why:** no Wesness event was found after 19 Jul 2026, and the stated expansion focus (Delhi and the north-east) has no Wesness presence on record (P-13, P-20, P-21, P-31).
3. **Westside already announced three national Dance Off challenges at FRONT ROW.** **It could publish the cities and dates now and stage each heat at or beside a Westside store.** **Why:** two weeks after launch no cities, dates or venues are public, and over 90% of Westside revenue is made in stores (online is just over 6%) (P-38, P-63).
4. **Westside already uses YNG designers to rework its own labels** (Bombay Paisley and E.T.A in Sep 2025) and promises the 30 New Faces campaign roles. **It could tie every YNG and New Faces piece to a named label and a named rail in store.** **Why:** Parti says the youth labels are growing about 30% and Gen Z customers are up 44%, but YNG is reported only in views and shares (P-22, P-23, P-32, P-39, P-66).
5. **Westside already publishes festive edits** (the burnt-orange Diwali 2025 guide and "9 Days 9 Colours" for Navratri 2026). **It could run one named festive story from Navratri through Dussehra (20 Oct) to Diwali (8 Nov) and on into the wedding season, carried in store and on social.** **Why:** no Diwali film was found for 2025, and no Durga Puja or Diwali 2026 communication was visible on 16 Oct. The current edits are search pages, not a story (P-01, P-02, P-36, P-37, P-46).
6. **Westside already has a described voice**: "celebrates imperfections", "a safe space … without judgement". **It could write one brand line that sits over Wesness, YNG, FRONT ROW and the festive edits.** **Why:** each platform carries its own name, and no umbrella line has been found since "Endless Possibilities" (about 2011). Today the voice lives in agency and executive descriptions, not in public lines (P-47, P-49, P-53).
7. **Westside already opens stores at pace**: +50 net in the year to 30 Sep 2026, 60 opened in FY26, and about 50 a year guided at the AGM. **It could give every opening the same local launch format**: local creators, a Wesness promo run from the new store, and a WestStyleClub sign-up. **Why:** we found almost no public launch communication for the roughly 40 openings in Q3 and Q4 FY26, and growth is coming from new stores while like-for-like growth is weak (P-33, P-34, P-43, P-44, P-64, P-70).
8. **Westside already designs 400-500 pieces a week with 30-day lead times on trend items.** **It could tell shoppers what is new, and in which store, each week.** **Why:** this speed has only been told to investors and Bloomberg, while shoppers' own "new arrivals … starting at 199" haul videos already do this job unofficially (P-03, P-30).
9. **Westside already has 11.85 mn active WestStyleClub members, who drove over 90% of revenue in FY25.** **It could make membership the RSVP and entry key for Wesness, FRONT ROW heats and store events.** **Why:** the communities and the loyalty programme are reported separately, and no public link between them was found (P-12, P-22, P-60, P-61, P-62).
10. **Westside already used W.i.S.H. across a capsule (about Dec 2025) and a live set (Feb 2026).** **It could plan each collaboration as a dated arc**: announcement, drop, in-store appearance, then content. **Why:** even the capsule's launch date is not publicly clear (we estimated it from page metadata), and trade-press pickup was thin (P-09, P-12).
