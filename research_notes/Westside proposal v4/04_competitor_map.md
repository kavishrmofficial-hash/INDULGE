# Competitor map for Westside, mid-October 2025 to 16 October 2026

Prepared for Mask360's pitch to Westside (Trent Ltd). Window: 16 Oct 2025 to 16 Oct 2026. Anything dated before 16 Oct 2025 is marked **background**. Trent's own brands are left out on purpose. Indian FY: FY26 = Apr 2025 to Mar 2026; Q1 FY27 = Apr to Jun 2026; Q2 FY27 = Jul to Sep 2026.

## Summary (10 lines)

1. Rivals fall into three groups. Pantaloons, Max, Trends and Myntra run celebrity films and contests. H&M and Uniqlo run culture and in-store experiences. Shoppers Stop runs loyalty and personal service. Lifestyle, Azorte and M&S India were quiet, or at least left nothing in our research.
2. Uniqlo did the most in stores. It opened its 18th India store (Mar 2026) with Khushi Kapoor and local creators, then Pune (15 May), Bengaluru Phoenix Marketcity (5 Jun) and Thane (16 Oct). It reopened Gurugram as a 28,000 sq ft Super Large Format store (2 Oct), ran a UT pop-up with limited drops, and ran a fleece campaign with Kriti Sanon and Jasprit Bumrah. All of this is unverified.
3. H&M ran the only multi-city programme that took a culture platform into physical spaces: Lollapalooza merchandise in select stores (Jan 2026), HYBE India "Icons in the Making" pop-ups in six cities (May to Jun 2026), and Sharvari as H&M Beauty's first India ambassador (Oct 2026). All unverified.
4. Shoppers Stop is the only rival that publishes store-level results. It reported customer footfall up 6% in a quarter covered by a May 2026 broker note, says personal shoppers bring in about a quarter of sales, and reportedly puts 50% of its marketing budget into hyperlocal activation. Each figure has a single source and is unverified.
5. Pantaloons signed its first-ever brand ambassador, Samantha Ruth Prabhu, for the Oct 2025 festive campaign "Spark Your Imagination". Its owner is ABFRL, not Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands, per our earlier notes. Max's ambassador films ran just before the window. Trends ran a spend-to-win holiday contest with Aneet Padda (20 Apr to 17 May 2026).
6. Myntra leads on creators. Creator-led shopping is said to be about 10% of revenue, with 500K+ active creators a month, and its Instagram affiliate programme passed 1M sign-ups (reported 14 Oct 2026). Its films are celebrity humour built around sale events.
7. Almost no rival publishes campaign results such as views, impressions or footfall, and we found no awards for any of this work. Shoppers Stop's footfall figure is the only exception.
8. We found dated store counts for only three rivals: Uniqlo (18 in Mar 2026, about 21 by 16 Oct 2026 by our arithmetic), H&M (a 64th store, date unconfirmed) and Lifestyle (125 in Jan 2025, out of date). For Shoppers Stop, Max, Pantaloons, Trends, Yousta, Azorte and M&S India, pull counts from their Q2 FY27 results before the pitch.
9. Rivals open a handful of stores loudly. Westside opened about 50 in the year, quietly. Its edge is reach (311 stores), exclusivity (its own labels) and member data (11.85 mn active WestStyleClub members). Every "where Westside wins" row below uses those three to tie content and creators to store visits that can be measured.
10. Limits: this pass could not run a new search or read any page, because the shared search budget was used up and every host was refused. Every competitor fact here comes from the 15 Oct 2026 notes, which were built from search snippets. All of it is marked unverified until someone opens the source.

## Westside baseline used in this map

All of these come from note 01 (16 Oct 2026). Note 01 itself marks them unverified against the primary filings.

- **311 stores on 30 Sep 2026**, up 50 in a year (Trent Q2 FY27 business update, 6 Oct 2026, via Motilal Oswal). [C-01]
- **97 cities and 7.15 mn+ sq ft on 30 Jun 2026**, an average of about 23,750 sq ft per store (our arithmetic). [C-02]
- **Almost all own-label.** BW Businessworld (2026) calls it a "100 per cent private-label model". [C-04]
- **11.85 mn active WestStyleClub members in FY26**, up 53%. [C-03]
- **Online "over 6%" of Westside revenue in Q1 FY27**, so roughly 94% of sales happen in stores (our arithmetic). [C-05]
- **Guidance of about 50 new stores a year** (AGM, 23 Jun 2026). [C-06]
- **Audience:** the core shopper is the urban woman. Westside also addresses young creatives through YNG and FRONT ROW (note 03).
- **Peer growth:** Bernstein (via Business Standard, 29 Mar 2026) said Westside grew faster than Lifestyle, Shoppers Stop and Pantaloons, even at its slower pre-FY26 pace. [C-07]

## 1. Shoppers Stop

**Stores (dated).** Not in our research. Pull the count from the Q2 FY27 investor presentation (Jul to Sep 2026) when it is out. We did not find a results date.

**Price position and core audience.** A premium multi-brand department store selling national and international labels, with a large beauty business. It is built around First Citizen, which it calls "the country's longest running loyalty program" [C-12, background], and around personal shoppers. This description is ours, not sourced in this pass.

**Marketing in the window.**
- **Valentine's, 30 Jan to 15 Feb 2026:** "Gifts of Love" positioned Shoppers Stop as a gifting destination with 500+ premium brands. Seen in a search summary only, so unverified. [C-08]
- **Apr 2026:** "The Travel Edit", a destination-led fashion campaign with styling guidance in store and online (IGDS, 22 Apr 2026). [C-09]
- **Hyperlocal and service:** it reportedly puts 50% of its marketing budget into hyperlocal activation, which it says drives 40% of business from new customers. Its Personal Shopper programme is tied to four loyalty tiers, and its stated FY26 focus is "experience, engagement and exclusivity". The article date is not confirmed. [C-10]
- **Agency:** Havas reportedly holds the creative mandate (Jun 2026). This comes from exchange4media tag-page teasers only. [C-11]
- **Background (Aug 2025):** First Citizen Club Fest, 20 to 31 Aug 2025, offering ₹3,500 of welcome benefits to new members. [C-12]
- **Not found:** a Diwali 2025 or Diwali 2026 campaign, a celebrity ambassador, or a content series.

**Results.**
- Customer footfall up 6%, described as its first positive growth in entries in several years. The source is a broker note dated May 2026, so the quarter is probably Q4 FY26 (Jan to Mar 2026). Unverified. [C-13]
- Personal shoppers: ₹273.5 crore of ₹1,094 crore revenue in one quarter (25%), and ₹1,200+ crore, or 26% of sales, for FY26. The quarter label is uncertain and is probably the quarter to 30 Jun 2025. [C-14]

**Audience vs Westside.** Both chase the urban woman with money to spend. The difference is that a Shoppers Stop customer comes for other people's brands and beauty, while a Westside customer buys a house look. The buyers overlap, but the reason for the visit does not.

**What they did.** Shoppers Stop ran seasonal edits ("Gifts of Love", Jan to Feb 2026; "The Travel Edit", Apr 2026) through stores and digital, backed by loyalty tiers, personal shoppers and a reported 50% hyperlocal budget. It is the only rival with a published footfall figure (+6%, in a quarter reported in May 2026).

**Where Westside can win.** Launch a bookable "Westside stylist" session for WestStyleClub members in the largest stores and film it as a weekly series. A styled own-label look is something Shoppers Stop's multi-brand personal shoppers cannot make exclusive.

## 2. Lifestyle (Landmark Group)

**Stores (dated).** 125, when it opened at Phoenix Palladium, Mumbai, in Jan 2025, with a plan for at least 50 new stores in 3 to 4 years (IndiaRetailing, 13 Jan 2025). This is **out of date**, and no 2026 count was found. [C-15]

**Price position and core audience.** A mid-market to mid-premium multi-brand department store for families, mostly in malls, selling both its own labels and third-party brands. This description is ours, not sourced in this pass.

**Marketing in the window.** None found. The "Dil Se Diwali" work credited to VML is undated. [C-16]
- **Background, do not quote:** a 2018-19 Facebook campaign is said to have driven 153,707 extra store visits across 14 stores. The only source is a secondary blog, and the audit report recommends dropping it. [C-17]

**Results.** None found for the window.

**Audience vs Westside.** Lifestyle is the closest like-for-like: a large mall format, a family shopper, and a mix of western and ethnic wear. The main difference is that Lifestyle also sells outside brands.

**What they did.** We found no dated campaign, ambassador, content series or store-launch marketing between Oct 2025 and Oct 2026. The last dated fact is the 125th store in Jan 2025.

**Where Westside can win.** In the cities where both chains trade, claim the mall department-store occasion calendar (wedding season, Diwali) with store-anchored occasion weekends promoted to WestStyleClub members. Lifestyle left these moments open in our research.

## 3. Max Fashion (Landmark Group)

**Stores (dated).** Not in our research.

**Price position and core audience.** Value fashion for the whole family. This description is ours, not sourced in this pass.

**Marketing in the window.** No campaign was found inside the window. Just before it (background):
- **Aug 2025:** Siddhant Chaturvedi was named Max's first male brand ambassador, fronting "How New Is Your New?" (The Week, 20 Aug 2025). [C-18]
- **Sep 2025:** the same platform ran with Kalki Koechlin and stressed **weekly new style drops**. The ambassador trio is Kalki Koechlin, Alaya F and Siddhant Chaturvedi. [C-19]

**Results.** None found.

**Audience vs Westside.** Max's customer is more price-led and more often in smaller cities. They may buy basics at Max and occasion wear at Westside.

**What they did.** Max built a three-celebrity ambassador platform, "How New Is Your New?", around weekly new drops (Aug to Sep 2025, just before the window). We found nothing dated after that.

**Where Westside can win.** Max says "new every week" in celebrity films; Westside can show newness instead, with a weekly "New In at Westside [store]" reel shot on the floor of named stores and sent to members in that store's catchment.

## 4. Pantaloons (ABFRL)

**Ownership note.** The brief lists Pantaloons under Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands. Our earlier notes, and a footfall-vendor client list, place Pantaloons under **Aditya Birla Fashion and Retail Ltd (ABFRL)**. Our background understanding is that Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands is the entity demerged in 2025 to hold the Madura brands, and that Pantaloons stayed with ABFRL. This was not re-checked online, so confirm it before the pitch. [C-20]

**Stores (dated).** Not in our research. Pull the count from ABFRL's Q2 FY27 investor presentation.

**Price position and core audience.** A value-to-mid department store for families, selling mostly its own labels alongside some outside brands. This description is ours, not sourced in this pass.

**Marketing in the window.**
- **Oct 2025:** Samantha Ruth Prabhu was named the brand's **first-ever brand ambassador**, launching the festive campaign "Spark Your Imagination" around a new proposition of "inspiring modern India to create their own fashion stories" (IMPACT; MediaNews4U). We did not capture the exact day. [C-21]
- **Background (Sep 2025):** the Durga Pujo campaign "Hok Tomar Agomon" ("Celebrate Your Arrival"). [C-22]
- **Not found:** any 2026 Pantaloons campaign.
- **Store tech:** Agrex.ai lists Pantaloons among clients of its CCTV-based footfall analytics. This is the vendor's own undated claim. [C-23]

**Results.** None found.

**Audience vs Westside.** Pantaloons' family shopper is more price-led and sits more in tier 2 and tier 3 cities. Westside is expanding into those cities too, so the overlap will grow.

**What they did.** Pantaloons signed its first-ever brand ambassador, Samantha Ruth Prabhu, for the Oct 2025 festive campaign "Spark Your Imagination". We found no dated work after that.

**Where Westside can win.** Answer one national celebrity with many local faces by casting YNG and "30 New Faces" talent from each opening city as the face of that store's launch.

## 5. Reliance Retail fashion formats: Trends, Yousta, Azorte

**Stores (dated).** We found no per-format counts for Trends, Yousta or Azorte. A plan for 200 to 250 Yousta stores was reported in 2024 (background). [C-27] Check Reliance's latest quarterly release, which may give only a combined fashion and lifestyle total.

**Price position and core audience.**
- **Trends:** mass-market value fashion with wide reach into smaller cities. This description is ours.
- **Yousta:** Gen Z value. "All products under ₹999, most under ₹499", with self-checkout and charging stations, and the range also sold on AJIO and JioMart (Storyboard18; FashionNetwork, Nov 2024). [C-27]
- **Azorte:** Reliance's mid-premium format and the most direct Reliance rival to Westside. No 2025-26 facts were found. [C-28]

**Marketing in the window.**
- **Trends, 20 Apr to 17 May 2026:** "This Holiday, Trend It", fronted by Aneet Padda. Shoppers who spent ₹1,499 or more entered a draw with 100+ winners of holidays (Passionate in Marketing). [C-24]
- **Trends, Onam:** a campaign by PlusOne Design featuring actor Preity Mukundhan, dancer Sarangi Shyam and DJ Attakkari, including the **Trends x Aarke Ritu Kumar** collection. We could not confirm the year (2025 or 2026). It falls inside the window only if it ran in 2026. [C-25]
- **Trends:** creators promote a wedding-card offer of at least 15% off in store. Undated. [C-26]
- **Yousta and Azorte:** no campaign found.

**Results.** None found.

**Audience vs Westside.** Trends and Yousta customers are value-first. Many Westside customers also shop there for basics, but for a different job. Azorte targets young urban mid-premium shoppers, closer to Westside's younger audience, but we have no evidence of its scale.

**What they did.** Trends ran a spend-to-win holiday contest with Aneet Padda (20 Apr to 17 May 2026) and a designer tie-up with Aarke Ritu Kumar around Onam (year unconfirmed). We found no marketing for Yousta or Azorte.

**Where Westside can win.** Do not match Reliance's spend-to-win and discount mechanics. Give members hooks that are not price-led, such as early access to a drop or a free styling slot, which keep Westside's no-discount stance intact.

## 6. H&M India

**Stores (dated).** 64. A Dehradun opening was reported as the **64th India store**, alongside Surat and tied to a Holiday Collection (Apparel Resources). **The date is unconfirmed**; it may be late 2025. [C-34]

**Price position and core audience.** International fast fashion at mid prices for younger, trend-led shoppers. H&M Beauty was added in 2025-26. This description is ours.

**Marketing in the window.**
- **About Oct 2025:** H&M reopened its DLF Mall of India (Noida) store with Festive 2025 Drop 2 and the H&M Beauty concept, billed as the first of a series of "reimagined" stores (Indian Retailer). We did not see a date on the page. [C-29]
- **From Nov 2025:** H&M fashion and H&M Beauty went on sale on Nykaa and Nykaa Fashion (FashionUnited UK, 29 Oct 2025). [C-30]
- **Jan 2026:** "The Sound of Style", the official Lollapalooza India 2026 merchandise collection, fronted by the teen boy band OutStation with pop artist Mary Ann. It was sold on HM.com, on Myntra and in **select stores** (EventFAQs, 8 Jan 2026). 2026 was H&M's second year as festival partner. [C-31]
- **30 Apr to 31 Jul 2026:** "ICONS IN THE MAKING". H&M was primary partner of the HYBE India audition to form "India's first global girl group". It ran pop-ups and experiential spaces with H&M Beauty stations in **Guwahati, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Delhi** from May to June, and online auditions closed on 31 Jul 2026 (FashionUnited India, 30 Apr 2026; IMPACT). [C-32]
- **Oct 2026:** H&M Beauty named actor **Sharvari** its first India ambassador for one year, with a festive makeup edit sold in H&M stores and on Nykaa and Myntra (Indian Retailer Brand License; Global Cosmetics News). [C-33]
- **Background (late Sep 2025):** a 10-year India anniversary event in Mumbai unveiled the Festive 2025 collection and a first look at H&M Beauty, with Samantha Ruth Prabhu, Bhumi Pednekar and Harsh Varrdhan Kapoor. [C-35]

**Results.** No footfall, sales or view figures were found for the HYBE pop-ups or the Lollapalooza merchandise.

**Audience vs Westside.** H&M's shopper is younger, more western-wear and more music- and pop-culture-led. That overlaps with the young creatives Westside targets through YNG and FRONT ROW, not with its core woman shopper.

**What they did.** H&M turned music and talent platforms into physical visits: Lollapalooza merchandise in select stores (Jan 2026) and HYBE India "Icons in the Making" pop-ups in six cities (May to Jun 2026). It then added a beauty ambassador, Sharvari, in Oct 2026.

**Where Westside can win.** Take FRONT ROW's dance-off heats and New Faces castings into Westside stores, starting in the six cities H&M toured, with a capsule rack sold only in store. That way the culture platform ends at the till.

## 7. Uniqlo India

**Stores (dated).** The **18th India store** opened at Phoenix Mall of Asia, Bengaluru, in **Mar 2026**. [C-36] Since then three new stores were reported: Pune (15 May), Bengaluru Phoenix Marketcity (5 Jun) and Thane (opening 16 Oct 2026). Gurugram Ambience was a refurbishment, not a new store. That gives **about 21 stores on 16 Oct 2026** if Thane opened and nothing closed. This is our arithmetic and unverified. [C-46]

**Price position and core audience.** LifeWear basics and functional clothing for men and women who pay for quality. In 2023 its India CEO said "the price versus value [proposition] is very important and our prices are not so low" (Business Today, Oct 2023; background). [C-44]

**Scale.** FY25 (Apr 2024 to Mar 2025): revenue above ₹1,100 crore (+44%) and profit of ₹178.4 crore. FY24: revenue ₹814.84 crore and profit ₹85.17 crore (Apparel Resources; reprinted by MKMA, 11 Mar 2026). [C-45] For the nine months to 31 May 2026, Fast Retailing reported double-digit revenue and profit growth in regions including India, without an India figure. [C-47]

**Marketing in the window.**
- **16 Mar 2026:** Jasprit Bumrah announced as brand ambassador on a multi-season deal. **We never found the source page.** [C-42]
- **Mar 2026, Mall of Asia opening:** actor Khushi Kapoor visited and met local creators and customers (Indian Retailer). [C-36]
- **15 May 2026:** Pune Phoenix Marketcity opened at about 21,000 sq ft (Punekar News; Retail4Growth). [C-37]
- **15 May to 15 Jun 2026:** a UT pop-up at Ambience Mall, Gurugram, with Disney, manga, anime and gaming graphic tees, **limited-time drops**, and visitors encouraged to post about it (Indian Retailer). [C-41]
- **5 Jun 2026:** Bengaluru Phoenix Marketcity opened at about 21,000 sq ft, its largest store in the city (Franchise India). [C-38]
- **2 Oct 2026:** Gurugram Ambience reopened after refurbishment at about 28,000 sq ft with 330+ styles, as India's second "Super Large Format" store (Indian Retailer). [C-39]
- **16 Oct 2026:** Thane Lake Shore store opening (Indian Retailer). [C-40]
- **About Oct 2026:** a fleece campaign with Kriti Sanon and Jasprit Bumrah across connected TV, print, digital, outdoor and **in-store**. The marketing director said Uniqlo is working with more regional and local creators to explain product function and styling, puts about 15% of media into connected TV, and has ruled out quick commerce. Single source (Storyboard18). [C-43]

**Results.** No figures for footfall, views or impressions were found for openings or campaigns.

**Audience vs Westside.** Uniqlo's customer buys basics and function, includes many men, and shops for a wardrobe staple rather than an outfit or an occasion. Westside's ethnic and occasion wear is largely outside Uniqlo's range.

**What they did.** Uniqlo made each opening a local event: Khushi Kapoor and local creators at its 18th store (Mar 2026), then new stores in Pune, Bengaluru and Thane, plus a 28,000 sq ft Gurugram reopening (2 Oct 2026). Alongside this it ran a UT pop-up with limited drops and a celebrity fleece campaign that ran in store.

**Where Westside can win.** Uniqlo turned about four openings into local events; Westside opens about 50 stores a year, so a standard creator-hosted launch kit at every opening, measured by store entries over the first eight weeks, can beat it on scale.

**Stores (dated).** Not in our research.

**Ownership.** M&S runs in India through a joint venture with Reliance Retail. This is our background understanding and was not re-checked in this pass. [C-48]

**Price position and core audience.** A premium British brand selling essentials: workwear, lingerie, kidswear and classic clothes, to an older, higher-income shopper. This description is ours.

**Marketing in the window.** Nothing found. The only M&S India mention in our research is a footfall-analytics vendor (Agrex.ai) naming M&S India as a client (undated, the vendor's own claim). [C-23]

**Results.** None found.

**Audience vs Westside.** M&S India's shopper is older and more premium. They overlap with Westside's working-woman and family buyer on essentials but rarely on ethnic or festive wear.

**What they did.** We found no dated campaign, opening or content from M&S India in the window. Its activity is unknown, not proven absent.

**Where Westside can win.** If a manual check confirms M&S India is quiet, Westside can lead store-level content on the working woman's wardrobe and everyday essentials with its own labels, at a lower price and with member benefits.

## 9. Myntra (plus Nykaa Fashion and AJIO)

**Stores (dated).** Myntra has no stores. Its quick-fashion service M-Now runs from dark stores, and reports disagree on how many. YourStory put it at about 80 within a year, with M-Now making up about 10% of orders in its live locations (Nov 2025). Apparel Resources reported 10 cities and 87+ dark stores (undated). A vendor blog (Fynd, 2026) says 10+ cities, 940+ pincodes and 90+ dark stores. [C-56]

**Price position and core audience.** An online marketplace driven by sale events, with a Gen Z and young-millennial core that is increasingly outside the metros. Myntra says 70%+ of its new customers in 2025 came from non-metro cities (Apparel Resources, undated). [C-57]

**Marketing in the window.**
- **Feb 2026:** Birthday Blast, 3rd edition. Humour films with Chunky Pandey, Nora Fatehi, Farhan Akhtar and Anu Malik, plus a Jeetendra social film (IMPACT; MediaNews4U). [C-49]
- **May 2026:** End of Reason Sale, 24th edition. Two humour films with Ranbir Kapoor (shirts) and Kiara Advani (dresses) (IMPACT). [C-50]
- **May 2026:** Myntra Beauty's "Beauty Made Personal", three films with Alia Bhatt (MediaNews4U). [C-51]
- **May 2026:** an affiliate feature inside **Ultimate Glam Clan**, its creator platform, paying about 8% to 12% commission by tier, unlocked after a creator's first three posts (afaqs; Apparel Resources). Reported Ultimate Glam Clan user totals conflict: 6M in one source, 8M+ in another. [C-52]
- **May 2026:** a push for small-town creators, a second edition of Creator School, and 3x creator earnings on festive styles ahead of the Big Fashion Festival (Business Standard, May 2026; Buzzincontent). [C-55]
- **2026:** the CMO says creator-led shopping is about 10% of Myntra revenue, and that active creators grew from about 100K a month to more than 500K (Social Samosa; company claim). [C-53]
- **14 Oct 2026:** the affiliate programme went live on Instagram with "over 1M sign-ups in a month" and 200K creators already making content. On Facebook it has 1.3M sign-ups and 650K+ active creators, with about 3M posts across the two platforms (Buzzincontent; Passionate in Marketing). [C-54]
- **Background (Sep 2025):** the Big Fashion Festival campaign with Ranbir Kapoor, Tripti Dimri and Karan Johar. [C-59]
- **Not found:** the Big Fashion Festival 2026 campaign (Sep to Oct 2026). It very likely ran, but our research did not reach it. [C-60]

**Nykaa Fashion.** Its notable event in the window was H&M fashion and H&M Beauty arriving on Nykaa and Nykaa Fashion from Nov 2025 (see H&M). We found no dated Nykaa Fashion campaign, store or content series. [C-30]

**AJIO.** Big Bold Sale 2026 went live on 27 May, with Shraddha Kapoor films selling **same-day delivery** as "fashion that can keep up with spontaneity" (Ascendants). This is a delivery-speed message, not store or content work. [C-61]

**Results.** These are company claims, not campaign results: about 10% of revenue creator-led, 500K+ active creators a month, and 1M+ Instagram affiliate sign-ups. We found no view, impression or award figures for the films.

**Audience vs Westside.** Myntra's shopper buys online, responds to sales and is increasingly in smaller cities. Many Westside customers also use Myntra, but for discounted brands and convenience. Westside's sales are still about 94% in stores.

**What they did.** Myntra ran celebrity humour films around its sales (Birthday Blast, Feb 2026; End of Reason Sale, May 2026) and scaled creator commerce, ending the window with an Instagram affiliate programme reported to have passed 1M sign-ups (14 Oct 2026).

**Where Westside can win.** Westside cannot match Myntra's online creator scale, so it should pay creators for store visits instead. Give YNG and local creators store-specific codes that are redeemed at the till and linked to WestStyleClub IDs.
