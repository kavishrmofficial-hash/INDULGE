# Zara, channel by channel (social + app), 2024-2026

Research date: 15 October 2026. Method: web search only. Every direct fetch (fashionunited.com, modash.io, zara.com, instagram.com, hypeauditor.com, inditex.com, theimpression.com, wallpaper.com) was refused by the sandbox proxy (403 on CONNECT), so no page was read in full and I could not look at the @zara grid myself. Everything below comes from search-result summaries of the cited pages. The 200-search budget for this run ran out before I could check the last items (Zara's first US menswear store, the current state of @LivingZara). Those are listed under Gaps.

Labels: **[V]** confirmed by two or more independent sources, or by a company source. **[S]** single secondary source. **[U]** unverified or low-authority (vendor blog, student work, listicle). **[I]** my inference, not a sourced fact.

---

## 1. Takeaway

Zara runs a small set of named, recurring formats. Each one appears on the same day across its own surfaces: the Instagram grid, the app and web home page, store windows and, for big moments, one physical installation. The brand account stays quiet and magazine-like, with few or no words. A very large creator layer it does not author carries the try-on and haul volume: about 101.6K Zara-related TikTok posts in six months.

From 2024 to 2026 Zara has added five things:
- its own broadcast surface, Zara Streaming;
- an editorial section written by influencers, The Fold-er, launched June 2026;
- an AI utility, Try-On, launched December 2025;
- tentpole designer and auteur projects, each with a pop-up: 50th anniversary, Kate Moss, Galliano, Guadagnino/Baisi;
- store features that make the store a filming location: the Nanjing "fit check" studio, March 2025.

What Westside can copy is the grammar, not the budget:
- Name every format.
- Give each format an author and a volume number.
- Run the same frame on the grid, the app and the window.
- Keep creators on surfaces Westside owns.
- End every piece of content in a store action: book, reserve, collect, try on.

India is a white space. Zara India has no Indian account I could find. Its only local creative moment in the window was the October 2024 Diwali capsule "Reverie" with artist Jayesh Sachdev, built around a 10-ft tiger sculpture at Phoenix Palladium, Mumbai. I found no Zara India Diwali or wedding campaign in 2025 or 2026.

### 2.3 TikTok

- **Followers.**
  - 14.7M, which FashionUnited ES (3 Feb 2026, link above) calls the most-followed fashion brand on TikTok, ahead of Adidas (12.1M) and Shein (11.5M) [S].

- **Academic reads.**
  - Cristófol-Rodríguez and Carrasco-Santos (Visual Review, 2023): Zara "makes a great communicative effort, creates strategic content", with solid engagement. https://visualreview.net/revVISUAL/article/view/4605 [S]

  - Scrumball: about 101.6K Zara-related TikTok posts and about 30.9B views in the six months to Aug 2026; a typical post gets 10K-100K views. https://www.scrumball.com/brand/zara-influencers/tiktok [U, vendor].

  - Launched mid-Dec 2025 in the app only, first in markets including Mexico, the UK, Germany, the Netherlands and Italy. Spain followed in late Dec 2025 or early 2026 (sources conflict); the US in early 2026.

## 3. Inferences (mine, not sourced)

1. **The @zaraman launch is about 2019, not 2026.** Textilwirtschaft's 31.2M figure for the main account and The Fashionisto's March 2019 post agree on early 2019. The search tool's "2026" reading of that article is wrong.
2. **Zara's channel grammar is a hub with spokes, and the hub is Zara's own surface.** The app and web host the long-form work (Streaming, Le Dîner, The Fold-er, Try-On). Instagram is the cover, TikTok the backstage. Creators produce volume Zara does not have to author. Store windows and pop-ups are the physical cover.
3. **The 2024-26 change is creators brought inside the frame.** Zara still avoids the influencer-ambassador model. What changed is that influencers now appear on Zara's owned surfaces, under Zara's art direction (Evan Ross Katz on Streaming in 2025, The Fold-er in 2026), and celebrities appear as people Zara dresses (Bad Bunny) rather than paid endorsers.
4. **Live shopping in the West underperformed as teleshopping and was reframed as cinema.** Le Dîner, Dec 2025, has no 2026 sequel found. Lesson: the show has to be worth watching without the cart.
5. **Every tentpole has a single physical room.** Paris George V, the Marais hôtel particulier, La Pelota, the Palladium tiger. The room produces the imagery and the press; the global stores sell the product.
6. **Zara India runs global content almost unchanged.** Its one local move (Reverie) used an Indian artist rather than Indian celebrities or festive tropes. This leaves Diwali, wedding season and regional dressing open to Westside.
7. **The magazine grid is less about borders than about sequencing.** Zara posts in themed runs of the same model, set or palette, so any three-across row reads like a spread. This needs direct observation to confirm.
