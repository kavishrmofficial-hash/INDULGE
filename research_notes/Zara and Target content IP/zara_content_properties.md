# Zara content IP: the system of named, recurring properties

Research date: 15 October 2026. Prepared for the Mask360 x Westside pitch ("Seen at Westside").
Method: web search (standard and extended). WebFetch was blocked by the sandbox proxy (DNS failure on fashionunited.uk and wallpaper.com), so every finding below comes from search-result text, not from reading full pages. Credits from models.com and visie.io are login-gated listings seen only as snippets; treat them as indicative.

---

## 1. Takeaway

Zara does not run "campaigns" so much as a portfolio of named, numbered properties that behave like magazine franchises. Each one has a name, an edition number or volume, a season, a fixed creative team and a physical premiere.

- **One house, many labels.** A small, stable creative team gives every property the same look. Baron & Baron leads creative direction, Steven Meisel shoots, Karl Templer styles and creative-directs, Craig McDean shoots SRPLS and Beauty, Pat McGrath does make-up and Guido Palau does hair. Each sub-line then gets its own small identity: M/M Paris designed a typeface and seven symbols for Origins, SRPLS numbers its collections CLLCTN 01-16, and Galliano's line is Re{form} Vol. 1.
- **Portrait first, product second.** The signature image is a studio or plain-location portrait, often black and white. It shows no price and no offer, and the logo is the only branding. Films are genre pieces rather than ad cutdowns: a sci-fi teaser, a split-screen spoken-word film, a 43-minute Christmas film by Luca Guadagnino, a telenovela directed with Glen Luchford, a Super 8 film for Kate Moss.
- **Every big property premieres in a physical, time-boxed room** and then rolls out to selected stores and the app. Examples: Origins at 40 rue de Richelieu, the 50 Creators at 40 avenue George V, Galliano at 70 rue des Archives, Baisi Guadagnino at La Pelota in Milan, Chavarria at 73 Spring Street, Bad Bunny at Plaza Las Americas, and the Jayesh Sachdev tiger at Phoenix Palladium in Mumbai. This is the part Westside can copy most directly, because Westside's advantage is its store.
- **Culture calendar, not retail calendar.** Launches are timed to Paris and Milan fashion weeks, the Super Bowl, the Met Gala, Diwali (in India) and Zara's own birthday (9 May).
- **Scarcity and drops.** Limited quantities, Drop 1 and Drop 2, flagship-only availability (Galliano in the Madrid and London flagships), and on-demand video afterwards.
- **Owned channels over rented ones.** In the West, Zara Streaming runs inside Zara's own app and website rather than on a third-party platform, a choice reported as a way to keep control of the look. In June 2026 Zara added The Fold-er, a shoppable magazine section inside the app.

For Westside, the takeaway is to turn "Seen at Westside" into a set of named, numbered, crew-credited properties. Each should premiere in one store as an event and use one house look across all of them. Hiring Meisel is not the point; the system is.

## 2. Cited findings, by property

### 2.1 ZARA ORIGINS (elevated menswear basics with its own editorial identity)
- **Launch:** unveiled 15 Sep 2021 after two years of development. A lasting men's wardrobe of 205 pieces, sold online and in 39 international stores. Source: FashionNetwork FR, 15 Sep 2021, https://fr.fashionnetwork.com/news/Zara-monte-encore-en-puissance-a-paris,1334356.html
- **Premiere:** pop-up at 40 rue de Richelieu, Paris 1er, 27 Sep-5 Oct 2021, during Paris Fashion Week. Sources: same; FashionNetwork CA, 28 Sep 2021, https://ca.fashionnetwork.com/news/Zara-Origins-opens-pop-up-for-Paris-Fashion-Week-launches-worldwide-online,1338704.html
- **Edition 1 film:** an orchestral reimagining of Aphex Twin's "Avril 14th" by Bryan Senti, commissioned by the agency CANADA, directed by Roger Guardia and shot in a swimming pool. Sources: https://evolutionmusicpartners.com/bryan-senti-zara-origins/ ; https://adg-fad.org/laus/proyecto/zara-origins-avril-14th/ (c. 2021; exact date not confirmed). The Fall 2021 stills are credited to Tyler Mitchell, styled by Marq Rise (models.com snippet, Sep 2021).
- **Edition 2 (SS22):** a mixed-media project on Bob Marley's "Sun is Shining". The palette was indigo, fuchsia and lilac over neutrals, and linen was added. Source: The Fashionisto, 2022, https://thefashionisto.com/zara-origins-collection-2022-campaign
- **Edition 3 (Winter 2022):** Bynt was creative director. M/M Paris designed "an entirely new graphic identity", with a new typeface and seven symbols ("imagotypes") applied to branding and garments. The soundtrack was "Autumn" by Aoki Takamasa. The line became a year-round wardrobe topped up by limited seasonal pieces. Source: The Impression, 2022, https://theimpression.com/?p=10877908
- **Later credits (models.com snippets):** Bynt as photographer for 2024 (Apr 2024) and SS25 (Feb 2025). Valentin Hennequin as photographer with Juan Medina as creative director for FW25 (Oct 2025). Laurence Ellis as photographer with Joe McKenna styling for a July 2026 campaign (unverified). For FW26, Jegor Venned fronts an edit of workwear, striped shirts, knit polos and relaxed denim in indigo, navy, brown and cream. Source: The Fashionisto, Aug 2026, https://www.thefashionisto.com/?p=1523031
- **In store:** the Champs-Elysees flagship's men's floor has a central zone dedicated to "Origins". Source: FashionNetwork FR (date unclear), https://fr.fashionnetwork.com/news/Zara-devoile-son-nouveau-flagship-sur-les-champs-elysees,1508745.html

### 2.3 SRPLS (functional, military-surplus line, numbered collections)
- **Debut:** Nov 2018, online only, in weekly drops from 15 Nov. 50 pieces at £19.99-£400, sized XXS-XXL for men and women. The name comes from "surplus" (military uniform stock). Sources: Hypebeast, Nov 2018, https://hypebeast.com/2018/11/zara-srpls-2018-lookbook ; Refinery29, https://www.refinery29.com/en-gb/zara-military-collection ; Modaes, https://www.modaes.com/back-stage/zara-se-cuela-en-la-semana-de-la-moda-de-paris
- **Team:** Karl Templer's own site says he "currently serves as Creative Director for Zara SRPLS... oversees the entire creative vision" (https://karltempler.com/about, undated). Craig McDean has shot SRPLS repeatedly: CLLCTN 02 (Spring 2019), FW22, SS23, Spring 2024 and Fall 2024. Sources: The Impression, https://theimpression.com/zara-srpls-fall-2022-ad-campaign-review/ ; https://theimpression.com/zara-srpls-launches-spring-2024-collection/ ; models.com snippets.
- **Cadence:** biannual, numbered "CLLCTN" releases in two drops. The 13th collection (FW24) had Drop 1 on 7 Nov and Drop 2 on 14 Nov 2024, at $27.90-$329 (Refinery29, https://refinery29.com/en-us/zara-srpls-collection-fall-winter-2024). CLLCTN 16 (summer 2026) launched in April 2026, with a second drop on 16 Apr, a broader palette and washed finishes, for women, men and children (FashionNetwork, 9 Apr 2026, https://us.fashionnetwork.com/news/Zara-expands-its-srpls-range-for-next-summer,1822357.html). Grazia describes the concept as "beautility" (https://graziadaily.co.uk/fashion/shopping/zara-srpls-secret-collection).

## 3. Inferences (my reading, not sourced fact)

1. **The edition number is the IP.** Origins (editions 1-3), SRPLS CLLCTN 01-16, Atelier Collection 01-04, Van Duysen Collection 01-04, Re{form} Vol. 1, SR_A Season 2 and Zara Streaming episodes 1-3 all count. Numbering turns a product drop into a series people collect and wait for, and it lets the press write "the 16th" rather than "another".
2. **The credit list is part of the content.** Zara's credits (photographer, stylist, hair, make-up, casting, set) circulate on models.com, The Impression and Fashion Gone Rogue. Reviewers then cover the campaign as fashion work rather than advertising. For Westside, publishing credits for every Trial Room episode, and crediting local stylists and photographers by name, would make it read as editorial.
3. **Restraint signals premium.** Zara's top-tier images seem to omit price, offer, call to action and product description. The wordmark sits alone, often on black-and-white portraits or plain backgrounds, and the palette is declared per season (SS26: powder blue, sage, blush). I infer this from the campaign descriptions; I did not audit the posts. Westside's festive content should drop the "Upto X% off" layer from its hero assets.
4. **The physical premiere does the PR.** Every major property opens in a short, specific, by-appointment or exhibition-style room before it rolls out. This maps directly onto Mask360's "First Look" and onto Westside's 311 stores. A Westside "First Look" should be an event with a guest list and an exhibition layout, not a ribbon-cutting.
5. **Zara localised once in India with an artist and a sculpture** (Reverie, Diwali 2024, tiger at Phoenix Palladium). This is the closest precedent for what Westside could own nationally: an annual named Diwali artist edition.
6. **Zara's live streaming in the West appears to have stalled** after three episodes. Its luxury-host, director-made format is expensive and rare. Westside's "Style Call" (bookable one-to-one styling on WhatsApp) is a different, more scalable answer. Westside can claim intimacy rather than spectacle.
7. **Archive as a source of content.** Narciso Rodriguez's archive, Galliano reworking Zara's own past seasons, and the "ZARA MAN ARCHIVE" editorial all use the brand's own history as material. Westside has nearly three decades of own-label history. The founding year (reportedly 1998) needs verifying before it is used.
8. **Zara Man's in-house strands** ("New Gen", "Evening Edition", "Archive", "Speed Run", "Selected by") show that not every property needs a Meisel. A named, recurring in-house format with a consistent photographer can carry the house look at low cost. That model fits Westside's in-house team.
