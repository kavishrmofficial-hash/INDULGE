**Read this first (method and conventions)**
- Indian FY runs April to March. FY25 = Apr 2024 to Mar 2025. FY26 = Apr 2025 to Mar 2026. Q1 FY27 = Apr to Jun 2026. Q2 FY27 = Jul to Sep 2026. Q3 FY27 (Oct to Dec 2026) is the festive quarter now under way.
- **Source limits:** The research proxy blocked direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl returned DNS or 403 errors for business-standard.com, storyboard18.com, outlookbusiness.com and kalkine.co.in). Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Before anything goes on a client slide, check the key numbers against the primary Trent PDFs cited (docs.trent-tata.com, bseindia.com, nseindia.com).
- **Biggest structural caveat:** Trent does **not** publish Westside-only revenue or Westside-only like-for-like (LFL) growth. It reports standalone revenue (Westside + Zudio + others), store counts by banner, a fashion-portfolio LFL band (e.g. "low single digits"), and Westside's online share. Any Westside revenue figure is an analyst estimate or a derivation, and is labelled as such below.

## 1. Westside performance: revenue, growth, LFL, productivity, stores, cities (FY25, FY26, Q1 FY27, Q2 FY27)

### Takeaway
Trent's growth now comes almost entirely from new stores. Fashion-portfolio LFL has been low single digits or negative since early FY26: low single digits in Q1 and Q2 FY26, marginally negative in Q3 FY26, low single digits in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27. That is down from double-digit LFL in FY25, and revenue per sq ft fell 6-12% YoY in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27. Westside ended FY26 with 300 stores (52 net added) after a heavy Q4 opening wave. Openings then nearly stopped (1 net in Q1 FY27) before resuming with 10 net in Q2 FY27, taking the count to 311 on 30 Sep 2026. Q2 FY27 standalone revenue growth rose to 23% even though festivals fell in Q3 this year.

### Inferences
- **LFL is the core marketing problem.** Same-store growth has been low single digits or negative for five straight reported quarters (Q1 FY26 to Q1 FY27). Revenue per sq ft fell 6-12% YoY in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27. Footfall and conversion in mature stores, not store count, is where marketing can add the most. The decline in revenue per sq ft also reflects dilution from new stores still ramping up.
- **Q2 FY27 looks better, but festivals sat in Q3.** The 23% headline came without the festive season, which suggests underlying demand improved. LFL is undisclosed, though, and the Q2 FY26 base was soft. Q3 FY27 (Diwali quarter) results, expected around early January 2027 (business update) and February 2027 (full results), will be the real test. This timing is inferred from past reporting patterns.
- **Derived Westside FY26 revenue estimate (our derivation, not a disclosure):** If online was about Rs 300 cr and about 6% of Westside revenue (ICICI Direct, MD&A), Westside FY26 revenue was roughly Rs 5,000 cr. That would be about a quarter of Trent's Rs 19,701 cr standalone revenue. Online "over 6%" means total revenue could be somewhat below Rs 5,000 cr. Use as an order of magnitude only.
- **FY26 store-age mix:** 52 of the 300 FY26-end stores (about 17%) were under one year old, and 22 opened in Q4 alone. A large group of stores is still ramping in FY27, so new-store launch and catchment-awareness activity matters.

## 2. Store expansion: net additions, cities, formats, closures, FY27 plans

### Takeaway
Westside's network grew from 248 to 311 stores in 18 months. Additions are lumpy: 13, 17 and 22 net in Q2-Q4 FY26, then 1 and 10 in Q1-Q2 FY27, and it consolidates a handful of stores each year (24 in FY25). Guidance conflicts. At the 23 Jun 2026 AGM, management gave ~50 Westside stores a year and a ~700-store long-term potential. In a 20 Jul 2026 Bloomberg interview, Westside CEO Shailina Parti spoke of "up to 100" openings a year, funded partly by a Rs 2,500 cr raise. H1 FY27's 11 net additions are well behind either pace.
