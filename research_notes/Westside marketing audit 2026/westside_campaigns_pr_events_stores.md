## 3. Owned IPs and platforms (Wesness, YNG, FRONT ROW, Studiowest, collaborations)

### Takeaway
Westside runs three owned IPs. **Wesness** is a women's fitness community, about 25,000 strong in four cities, with formats anchored at its stores. **YNG** is a creator and talent community of about 4,000 members, reported at 6M views in Q4 FY26. **FRONT ROW**, launched 1 Oct 2026, is a culture platform aimed at music, dance and new talent. All three report engagement metrics, not store footfall.

### Cited Findings
**Wesness**
- About 25,000-member community across Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad and Bengaluru (Feb 2026). — [BSE press release, 10 Feb 2026](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/f98f2ffa-597c-49f2-a9e7-835cfe0dc5a7.pdf)
