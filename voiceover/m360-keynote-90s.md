# m360 OS: the 90 second keynote cut

Fast, punchy, keynote energy. About 200 words, roughly 90 seconds at ElevenLabs speed 1.1.
Every short phrase below is a cut in the film, so read it with a beat between phrases. The full stops are the beats.

---

## The script (paste into ElevenLabs)

**1. Hook**
Friday. Seven forty p.m.
A client asks: where are we on the reels?
Six WhatsApp groups. Three email threads. One very sad spreadsheet.
The work is happening. You just can't see it.

**2. Reveal**
So I built m360.
One operating system for the whole agency.

**3. The day**
Mood? On fire.
Check in, verified.
End of day: shipped, next, blocked.
Blocked two days in a row? It flags itself.
Nobody chases anybody.

**4. The week**
Monday, three outcomes.
Wednesday, the twenty percent check.
Friday, hit or miss.
And every revision gets counted.

**5. The game**
And it's a game.
Points for everything.
But shipping scores three times more than showing up.
Streaks. Levels. Trophies.
And a podium, every single week.

**6. The vibe**
Kudos. Wins. Polls. Birthdays.
Team chat. Shared music.
And an anonymous pulse, so I know how the team really feels.

**7. Clients and new business**
Every client has a brain, with the lines we never cross.
Radar reads the industry every morning.
New CMO? Tap Pursue. The pitch writes itself.
Twenty five thousand contacts. Just ask.

**8. The buddy**
And a buddy that shows every new joiner around. Out loud.

**9. Everything else**
Mail. Calendar. Drive. A browser. Notes. Leave. Hiring. A focus timer.
All inside.

**10. The founder's view**
And for me? One dashboard. Everything. Live.

**11. Close**
m360.
Results and culture. The only two things that count.
