## Company Overview
Indulge Global was launched in 2022 by Karan Bhangay and Advita Bihani and provides luxury lifestyle concierge service. The startup shot to fame after featuring on Shark Tank India Season 4.

## The Pitch
During the premiere of Shark Tank India Season 4, Karan and Advita pitched their business, seeking ₹50 lakh for 1% equity, valuing Indulge at ₹50 crore.

## Business Model
The startup's innovative approach to concierge services includes its 0% commission model and the availability of a 24/7 concierge team (referred to as "Genies"). It offers various concierge services such as personalised travel, lifestyle management, bespoke experiences, and more.

## Shark Feedback
Despite a compelling pitch, the founders did not secure a deal from any of the Sharks. The feedback highlighted several key issues:

- Aman Gupta and Peyush Bansal found the ₹50 Crore valuation to be too high for the current scale.
- Namita Thapar and Vineeta Singh expressed strong concerns about the ability to scale a human-dependent service model without compromising quality.
- Anupam Mittal questioned the transparency of the business model, particularly regarding commissions from brand partners.
- Peyush Bansal, who showed initial interest, backed out after learning that prominent investor Nikhil Kamath had previously invested and exited the company.

## Client Base
The brand has grown from serving just three ultra-high-net-worth individuals (UHNI) and their families in 2022 to catering to over 180 clients today. The startup's clientele includes high-profile industrialists, actors, and entrepreneurs like Kunal Shah, Washington Sundar, and Arjun Kapoor.
